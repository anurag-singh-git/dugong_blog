## New post

**Dispatch Nº 96 - "The pickup order that should have shipped"**
`how-to-change-a-shopify-order-from-local-pickup-to-shipping.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-07-31 · 2,362 words

Topic: wrong delivery method orders, specifically customers accidentally checking out with free
local pickup when they meant shipping, and Shopify's inability to convert the order afterward.
Covers the full complaint arc: checkout preselects the cheapest delivery option and local pickup
is free, so it arrives preselected and Shop Pay carries phone shoppers straight past the choice;
no setting exists to change the default, reorder the options, or make pickup opt-in; order
editing does not touch the delivery method and pickup/local-delivery orders often cannot be
edited at all; the supported fix is cancel, refund, rebuild as a draft order, and re-invoice; the
half workaround of buying a label anyway leaves a pickup order that still fires pickup
notifications and never collected a shipping fee; and the label-queue hazard of pickup orders
shipped by accident during bulk label buys. The playbook: read delivery method against address,
distance, and history, flag mismatches before fulfillment, ask the customer the same hour,
convert cleanly on a ship answer, confirm cleanly on a pickup answer, keep pickup orders out of
the label queue with a tag, and count wrong-method orders weekly to fix the checkout copy.
