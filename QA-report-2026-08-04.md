## Fixes applied

### 1. Related-grid mesh segments out of order, 92 inversions across 35 posts

The mesh convention (each publish inserts the new card at the top of every older post's related
grid, so the newer-than-self segment reads newest-first) held for card *presence* but not for
*order*. Re-deriving order from each card target's `datePublished` + dispatch number found 92
adjacent pairs sitting date-inverted across 35 grids:

| Pattern | Where |
|---|---|
| Nº 82 (JUL 12) above Nº 83 (JUL 15) | systemic, 33 grids |
| Nº 76-81 cluster scrambled (e.g. Nº 76 above Nº 78, Nº 78 above Nº 79, Nº 80 above Nº 81) | 24 grids, matches the 08-03 backfill's insertion positions |
| Nº 90 (JUL 24) above Nº 91 (JUL 25) | 1 grid (Nº 86) |
| Nº 70 above Nº 71 / Nº 72; Nº 50 above Nº 51 | 3 grids (Nº 55, Nº 50, Nº 49) |

Worst single page was Nº 50 (overselling) with 11 cards out of position. Fixed by reordering
**only the newer-than-self card blocks** into strict newest-first (date, then dispatch number),
keeping every card block byte-identical, keeping each post's launch-time curated older cards in
their original order below the mesh segment, and preserving all inter-card whitespace. Two
assertions ran per file before writing: card set unchanged, and byte multiset of the whole file
unchanged (797 lines moved, 797 lines landed, net zero). Post-fix verification: 0 order
violations, 0 contiguity breaks, 0 duplicate cards across all 54 wired grids.

### 2. Missing mesh card: Nº 76 never received Nº 77, a same-day pair

`how-to-set-a-minimum-order-amount-on-shopify.html` (Nº 76, 2026-07-05) carried no card for
`how-to-schedule-product-publishing-on-shopify.html` (Nº 77, same day, higher number), the one
older-links-newer edge every prior mesh check missed. Root cause worth recording: the ~35 oldest
posts write their ticker as literal `Nº` while newer posts use `N&ordm;`, so any check that
parses only one form drops the dispatch number for the other and same-date pairs collapse into
an unordered tie. Yesterday's "0 violations" and this sweep's own first pass both had that blind
spot; re-checking with both glyph forms surfaced the gap. Fixed by harvesting the majority card
variant already in circulation for Nº 77 (24 of 33 copies byte-identical) and inserting it at
the date-correct position, directly after the Nº 78 card, at the boundary of the mesh segment.
Mesh now verifies at **0 missing edges across all 1,431 older-to-newer pairs**.

### 3. llms.txt: one article entry missing its byline

The `How to block a customer on Shopify from placing orders` entry was the only one of 54
article entries ending without the standard `By Priya Singh, YYYY-MM-DD.` closer. Appended
`By Priya Singh, 2026-07-23.` matching the post's datePublished. All 54 entries now carry
uniform bylines (the three non-article resource links at the bottom rightly have none).
