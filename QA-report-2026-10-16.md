# QA report - 2026-10-16

Scheduled autonomous run. Full-tree QA of blog.dugong.live.

The 10-16 blog run finished at 09:47 UTC, two minutes before this audit opened, publishing
dispatch Nº 129, `how-to-handle-return-to-sender-orders-on-shopify.html`, and rewiring
sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, index.html, two hub pages and the author page.
The tree settled at 177 HTML files (94 rendered, 82 stubs plus 404.html) and did not move again
during the audit, so unlike the last two runs this is a single clean pass rather than a re-run.
Five defects found and fixed. Every edit was a read-modify-write against the live file, guarded by
an exact-occurrence-count assertion and a byte-delta assertion that abort the whole patch rather
than write a near-miss. No facts, FAQ answers, headlines or body structure were changed.

**New this run: the 38 external links were verified for the first time.** Standing item 8 has been
open since June because both the cloud sandbox and the desktop shell sit behind proxies that refuse
help.shopify.com, shopify.dev and wikipedia.org. This run went through the Claude desktop app's
built-in browser instead, which reaches them, and swept all 38 distinct URLs from the real origin.
Two were dead. That is defect 5 below, and it includes the `cancelling-orders` redirect first
flagged on 10-13 and unconfirmed ever since.

**Also new: the typography and contrast passes both ran on the real webfonts.** Inter, Hanken
Grotesk and JetBrains Mono were installed from the npm registry, served from local files, and the
Google Fonts request intercepted, so `document.fonts.check` is true for all three families on all
752 renders. Every layout, font-size, tap-target and contrast figure below is measured on the
typefaces the site actually ships.

## Method

- Structural audit of all 177 HTML files: tag and CSS brace balance, one h1 per rendered page,
  JSON-LD parses and field completeness, internal link targets, in-page and cross-page anchors,
  asset references, canonical vs og:url, og vs twitter parity, html lang, viewport, charset, meta
  description length and uniqueness, title length and uniqueness, the `| Dugong` suffix rule,
  duplicate ids, replacement characters and mojibake, unterminated entities, heading-level skips,
  img alt, stub noindex and redirect resolution, visible date vs JSON-LD datePublished, visible
  word figure vs JSON-LD wordCount, JSON-LD headline vs h1, dispatch-number sequence, visible em
  and en dashes.
- Headless Chromium render of all 94 rendered pages at 320, 360, 375, 414, 768, 1024, 1280 and
  1920 px with the real webfonts loaded (752 renders): horizontal page overflow, elements escaping
  the viewport, computed font size of every text-bearing element, broken images, tap-target height
  at phone widths, stranded separator glyphs in wrapped flex rows, console and page errors.
- WCAG AA contrast on every text node of all 94 pages at 375, 414, 768 and 1280 px, each measured
  against its resolved composited background with alpha blended through every ancestor: 144,830
  text nodes.
- Scroll-reveal completeness. The tree hides `.card`, `.section-head` and every `.article-body`
  paragraph, heading, `pre`, callout and blockquote behind `opacity: 0` and reveals them with an
  IntersectionObserver, so a reveal that never fires is invisible body copy. All 94 pages were
  scrolled end to end at reader pace at 375 and 1280 px and every element re-measured, then
  re-checked with JavaScript disabled and under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Cross-page card metadata: all 4,385 cards in the tree compared against the page each one links
  to, on reading time, tag class, tag text, date label and `datetime` attribute.
- Spelling pass widened this run. Instead of a fixed pair list, every word in the tree was tested
  against its US/UK twin across 22 suffix transformations (-ise/-ize, -isation/-ization,
  -yse/-yze, -our/-or, -ence/-ense, -tre/-ter, -ogue/-og, -lled/-led), and any spelling in the
  minority against its own twin was read in context by hand. This is what turned up defects 1
  through 4; three of the four were invisible to the old 22-pair list. Doubled-word check run per
  text node rather than across the flattened document.
- Coverage cross-check: sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt and the 83 `md/` renditions
  against the file tree; sitemap lastmod and feed pubDate sanity, feed ordering, guid uniqueness,
  XML well-formedness before and after every edit.
- Hub and author ItemLists: `numberOfItems` against real item count, every item URL resolved, every
  one of the 415 FAQ deep-links on the question index resolved to a real `#faq-N` id on a real page.
- External links: all 38 distinct URLs fetched from their own origin through the built-in browser,
  final resolved path compared against the requested path.
- Content: Nº 129's central factual claim checked against the primary source.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **Nº 129 shipped with UK spellings in its prose: `recognise` twice, `recognises` twice and
   `recognising` once.** Tree-wide the house spelling is unambiguous, 53 `recognize`, 45
   `recognized`, 32 `recognizes` and 4 `recognizing` against those five. Two of the occurrences are
   inside FAQ answers, which exist twice each, once in the visible copy and once in the FAQPage
   JSON-LD, so all copies were changed together and the JSON-LD re-parsed clean afterwards. Fixed
   in the HTML, in `md/how-to-handle-return-to-sender-orders-on-shopify.md` and in llms-full.txt.

2. **Nº 129 also used `fulfilment` five times, and mixed it against `fulfillment` eleven times on
   the same page.** The post is about a fulfillment-status enum, so the two spellings sit a
   paragraph apart throughout. Tree-wide there were no other instances against several hundred
   `fulfillment`. One copy had already travelled: the card dek on index.html and the item
   description in feed.xml both carried it. All five in-page occurrences plus index.html, feed.xml,
   the md rendition and llms-full.txt were changed together. None of the five is inside quotation
   marks; the one closest to a quote paraphrases an app builder's forum summary in the blog's own
   voice, which is the same test the tree already applies to Shopify's `Canceled` labels, where the
   vendor spelling is kept only because it is a quoted UI string.

3. **`Normalise` twice on the shipping-label-adjustments post**, once as a HowTo step `name` in the
   JSON-LD and once as the visible step label, against 25 US forms tree-wide. Pre-existing since
   that post shipped; invisible to every earlier run because `normalise` was not one of the 22 pairs
   they checked. Fixed in both places plus the md rendition and llms-full.txt.

4. **`personalised` once on the half-the-order-never-ships post**, against 102 `personalized`
   tree-wide. Fixed there and in the md rendition and llms-full.txt. The `Personalisation` on the
   missing-personalization post was checked and deliberately left: it is inside the quoted Shopify
   Community thread title "Blank Field for Customer Personalisation" and is reproduced as the forum
   spells it.

5. **Two external deep-links were dead, both soft 404s that bounce the reader to a parent index.**
   - `help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/cancelling-orders`, on the
     cancelled-orders post, returns 200 at `/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders`. Shopify
     renamed the page to the US spelling: `canceling-orders`, one L, which resolves directly.
     Repointed. The anchor text was left as written, since the destination is the document it
     always meant.
   - `help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/shopify-magic`, on the AI-product-descriptions
     post, returns 200 at `/en/manual/ai-powered-tools`. The Shopify Magic page is retired and the
     string "Shopify Magic" no longer appears anywhere in the Help Center; that section now lists
     Sidekick, media generation and Tinker. Repointed at the live `/en/manual/ai-powered-tools`
     hub, which is where the redirect already lands, so the link is now a direct 200 and no claim
     in the post changed. The branding staleness is a separate matter: see standing item 10.

   Both were fixed in the HTML (visible anchor plus the JSON-LD `url`) and in the md rendition.

## Content verification

Nº 129's central claim was checked against the primary source rather than taken from the blog run's
notes. The 2026-10 Admin GraphQL `FulfillmentEventStatus` enum has exactly the eleven values the
post lists, in the order it lists them, and no value meaning a parcel is coming back;
`FAILURE` is documented as "The fulfillment request failed", which is what the post says it is.
`RETURNING_TO_SENDER` and `BUYER_ACTION_REQUIRED` both exist in the unstable version and in no
stable one. The post's framing, that Shopify has written the word down and not shipped it, is
accurate as of today.

## Standing items (not changed)

1. index.html keeps an og:description that differs from its meta description. Both are
   hand-written page-level blurbs well inside the length caps. 404.html and priya-singh.html, which
   were on this list in earlier runs, now match; only index.html still differs.
2. Every headless render logs two console errors, both artifacts of the sandbox: a root-absolute
   favicon path that resolves on the live domain, and the Google Fonts stylesheet, whose upstream
   request the proxy blocks. The font request is answered locally, so the typography is verified;
   the logged error is the blocked upstream connection, not a site fault.
3. The three capitalized `Canceled` on the Flow page are still deliberate and still untouched: they
   are a verbatim enumeration of Shopify's documented Flow run statuses, and a quoted label is
   reproduced as the vendor spells it. Tree-wide the prose is `cancelled` / `cancelling`.
4. Two doubled words survive, both grammatical: "deciding what a customer is is not a slot to fill"
   in the repeat-customers post, and "double opt-in in particular" in the fake-accounts post.
   Neither is an error. Rewriting body copy on style grounds is not something an unattended run
   should do.
5. Micro-labels (tag pills at 10.24 px, card dates and bylines at ~11 px, footer headings and the
   ticker at 11.52 px) still render under 12 px at 768 px and wider. Deliberate editorial
   typography; the 12 px floor is a mobile rule and desktop was left alone. All of them clear AA
   contrast, confirmed on the real typefaces this run.
6. Inline links in body prose run 19 to 21 px tall at phone widths. That is correct: the 24 px
   minimum is for standalone controls, and there are none under it. Earlier runs reported these
   as tap-target hits; they are not.
7. The homepage ticker is an infinite marquee with no pause control. It is `aria-hidden="true"` and
   the tree honors `prefers-reduced-motion`, so it was left as is.
