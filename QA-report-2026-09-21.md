# QA report - 2026-09-21

Scheduled autonomous run. Full-tree QA after Dispatch Nº 121 ("The twenty-first order") shipped this
morning. Five defects found and fixed. No facts, FAQ answers or headlines changed.

## Method

- Structural audit of all 151 HTML files (77 rendered, 74 stubs): tag balance, one h1, JSON-LD parses,
  internal links and anchors, image sources, canonical, viewport, meta description length and
  uniqueness, duplicate ids, replacement characters, doubled words.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **Nº 121 overstated its length.** It claimed 1,900 words and 8 min. The body is 1,374 words; the
   1,900 figure included the FAQ, which no other post counts (siblings report body words at about
   250 wpm). Corrected to 1,400 words / 6 min in the ticker, article meta, BlogPosting `wordCount`
   and `timeRequired`, the index BlogPosting entry, the homepage card, and the related card in 73
   sibling posts.
2. **UK spelling in a US-English site.** Nº 121 used "fulfil", "fulfilment" and "mum"; Nº 120 used
   "fulfilment" twice. The rest of the site uses "fulfillment" hundreds of times. Normalised to US
   spelling in both posts and in index.html, feed.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt. FAQ visible text
   and FAQPage JSON-LD still match 5/5. A quoted thread title ("why can't we fulfil...") was left as
   quoted.
3. **Visible em dashes.** Swapped for the separator Nº 121 already uses: `<title>` suffix on 73 posts
   and the homepage now reads "| Dugong Field Notes" (same length, so no SEO effect); ticker
   "DISPATCH Nº 99 — AUG 3" style lines now use a middle dot; the closing CTA "runs it — calling
   your apps directly" now uses a comma (36 files); RSS link titles read "Dugong Field Notes RSS".
   The one remaining visible dash is the quote attribution "— A. Rao" on the homepage, left as
   standard typography. CSS comments were not touched.
4. **Dangling separator dots on phones.** The article meta row (tag, dispatch number, date, read
   time) and the homepage hero meta wrap on narrow screens and left a lone "·" at the end of a
   line. Separators are now hidden at 520 px and below; the flex gap still spaces the items.
5. **Byline squeezed on phones.** At 375 px "Priya Singh" broke onto two lines and the avatar
   could shrink. Name is now `nowrap` and the avatar `flex-shrink: 0`. The role still wraps.

Fixes 4 and 5 are one small CSS block (marked "QA 2026-09-21") in 77 rendered pages.
Also: llms.txt entries for Nº 121 and the Aug 7 "delivered but not received" post were missing the
"By Priya Singh, date." line every other entry carries. Added.
