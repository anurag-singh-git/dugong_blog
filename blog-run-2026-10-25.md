## New post

**Nº 136 - "How to chase overdue Shopify B2B invoices by company"**
`how-to-chase-overdue-shopify-b2b-invoices.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-25 - 2,967 words -
11 min read

Pain point: a trade account places a net 30 order, Shopify marks it payment pending, and on day 31
the order picks up an Overdue badge and nothing else happens. By the middle of the next month that
one account has four open invoices, the reminder settings have sent four separate emails about four
separate order numbers to the buyer rather than to whoever pays, and nobody on either side has ever
seen the total, because Shopify keeps no company balance anywhere.

Why this topic: high demand, almost no written mechanism. The open web has agency pages about
setting up B2B payment terms and app listings for chasing invoices. Nobody has put together that
overdue is not a value in `OrderDisplayFinancialStatus`, that the admin badge is drawn by comparing
a date to a clock so no webhook or Flow trigger can fire on it, that Flow's one relevant trigger
(`Payment schedule is due`) fires on day zero which a native reminder already covers, that Flow
cannot email customers at all, that `Company` carries total spent and no balance, or that
`PaymentSchedule.balanceDue` plus `completedAt` is the two-field query that produces the statement
the platform will not.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, Managing orders with deferred payments: four kinds of terms on a payment-pending
  order, due on receipt, due on fulfillment, net term and fixed term; net 7, 15, 30, 45, 60 and 90;
  "If the order isn't paid by the due date, then the order status becomes Overdue"; "You can create
  up to five payment reminders"; "Payment reminders can be set to the payment due date, or up to 30
  days after the due date"; "Payment reminders aren't sent for orders with purchase options such as
  subscriptions, preorders, or try before you buy."
- Shopify.dev, OrderDisplayFinancialStatus: eight values, AUTHORIZED, EXPIRED, PAID, PARTIALLY_PAID,
  PARTIALLY_REFUNDED, PENDING, REFUNDED, VOIDED. No overdue. This is the article's central fact: an
  unpaid net 30 order on day 5 and on day 95 carry the identical financial status.
- Shopify.dev, PaymentTerms: `overdue` is `Boolean!`, "Whether the payment terms have overdue
  payment schedules", alongside `dueInDays`, `paymentTermsName` and `paymentTermsType`.
- Shopify.dev, PaymentSchedule: `issuedAt` "Date and time when the invoice is sent"; `dueAt` "Date
  and time when the payment schedule is due"; `completedAt` "Date and time when the payment schedule
  is paid or fulfilled"; `balanceDue` "Remaining balance to be captured for this payment schedule";
  `totalBalance` "Remaining balance to be paid or authorized by the customer for this payment
  schedule"; `due` "Whether the payment schedule is due".
- Shopify.dev, Company: contacts, locations, draft orders, orders, `ordersCount`, metafields, note,
  `customerSince`, `totalSpent`. No outstanding balance, credit limit, DSO or aging field.
- Help Center, Shopify Flow triggers: `Payment schedule is due` exists under payment triggers. There
  is no overdue trigger and nothing at any interval after the due date. The four B2B triggers are
  Company created, Company location created, Company contact created and Company contact assigned
  permission.
- Shopify Community, 24 September 2026 (Ryan_Miller), "Anyone else stuck doing wholesale/net-terms
  invoicing manually since B2B opened up to non-Plus plans?": the post-order work "is still totally
  manual for most people". ecom-4all: reminders are per order, so a company with several overdue
  invoices gets "four separate emails instead of one statement of what they owe", and there is no
  per-company running balance in admin. Dortus: the running balance is "the bit everyone ends up
  rebuilding in a spreadsheet". sainthesam: reminders live under Settings > Notifications > Orders,
  up to five, excluded for subscriptions, preorders and try-before-you-buy, and "nothing stops an
  overdue company placing new orders". Five community replies, no staff reply, no accepted solution.
- Shopify Community, 27 August 2026 (UprightApps), "Selling B2B on net terms?": "Shopify marks the
  order as payment pending and moves on"; "The gap is that nothing fires before the due date, so the
  'heads up' email is still manual"; late B2B payment is usually "your invoice falling off a busy
  buyer's radar". sainthesam in the same thread on the only native credit lever, Customers >
  Companies > Checkout > submit all orders as drafts for review: "It parks new orders for your
  approval rather than refusing them, and someone has to switch it on and off by hand."
- Shopify Community, 1 October 2024 (z832), "Change in Order payment terms does not trigger Order
  update webhook or show in GET Order response": terms changed from due on receipt to net 90 in the
  admin with order create and order update both subscribed, no webhook arrived, and REST kept
  returning the old term. One speculative reply, no staff answer, no accepted solution.
- Shopify Community, open since 12 August 2022, "Can I use Flow to send out payment reminders to
  customers with payment pending?": DaveMcV, August 2022, "Currently, Flow can only send emails to
  internal/specific email addresses and not to Customers"; paul_n, August 2023, "Flow is not
  implementing a generic system to email customers outside of partners and Shopify Email"; KSabra,
  November 2023, asked for an accounts-payable email field so reminders reach AP as well as the
  buyer, never answered; DeeLT, October 2025, asked for reminders 7 and 14 days before a 30-day due
  date for schools paying on purchase orders, never answered. Still no accepted solution after four
  years.

Playbook shipped: read the due date, not the order status; roll every open balance up by company;
send one statement, not one email per order; copy the person who pays, not just the buyer; nudge
before the due date, not only after; hold new orders when an account crosses the line; age the list
and escalate the wording. Honest limits stated in the post: no automation makes a customer who has
run out of money pay; it cannot tell a staller from a lost invoice, so the first message assumes the
second; it has nothing to read if the terms were never put on the order; and if nobody owns the
receivable, the statement lands in an inbox nobody opens.

Overlap handled deliberately: Nº 101 (unpaid draft orders) and Nº 78 (sending invoices) sit one stage
earlier, before an order exists. This post links to both in prose and keeps its own weight on placed
orders under payment terms, the missing overdue status, the per-company roll-up and the credit hold.
