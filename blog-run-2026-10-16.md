## New post

**Nº 129 - "How to handle return to sender orders on Shopify"**
`how-to-handle-return-to-sender-orders-on-shopify.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-16 - 2,681 words - 9 min read

Pain point: a carrier turns a parcel around and nothing in the store says a word. The order stays
Fulfilled, the sale stays in the report, the units stay missing from the sellable count, and the
merchant finds out when the customer emails on day eleven or when somebody trips over the box.
Shopify has no status for a parcel coming back, no Flow trigger, no reship action, and it files the
event as a customer return, which quietly poisons return-rate reporting.

Why this topic: high commercial pain, very thin content on the open web. Everything written about
Shopify return to sender is either carrier help-desk copy for buyers or RTO-reduction listicles
aimed at the Indian COD market. Nobody has written about the missing enum value, nobody has
connected the reship workarounds to what each one breaks, and nobody has written about RTS as a
reporting problem. Search intent is strong and commercial: merchants looking for this are losing
money this week.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Admin GraphQL 2026-10, FulfillmentEventStatus: eleven values, ATTEMPTED_DELIVERY,
  CARRIER_PICKED_UP, CONFIRMED, DELAYED, DELIVERED, FAILURE, IN_TRANSIT, LABEL_PRINTED,
  LABEL_PURCHASED, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, READY_FOR_PICKUP. No returning-to-sender value. FAILURE is
  documented as "The fulfillment request failed", which is the fulfillment service failing, not a
  courier reversing. Verified against the 2026-10 page directly.
- The unstable version of the same enum adds RETURNING_TO_SENDER, "The fulfillment is being
  returned to the sender", plus BUYER_ACTION_REQUIRED. Neither is in a stable version. The
  Customer API 2026-07 and 2027-01 listings also lack both. So the word is drafted and unshipped.
- Shopify Community, 25 Aug 2024 (Shivam_Parikh), "Shopify Flow Based on Delivery Status": wants an
  automation once an order is delivered. Community reply suggests the fulfillment event created
  trigger. 31 Aug: poster reports it does not work because delivery happens roughly 48 hours after
  fulfillment, so the workflow never fires. No staff reply, thread unresolved.
- Shopify Community, 23 Jan 2020 (danalcohen), "Refulfilling a package that was Returned To
  Sender", six years long. Elias (Shopify Support), 25 Jan 2020: void the original label if it
  still qualifies, correct the address and buy a new one; otherwise duplicate the order with a 100
  percent discount, which keeps reports accurate but may require an inventory adjustment. 3 Oct
  2020, Elias again: voiding requires the label to be within 30 days and have no tracking activity.
  27 Apr 2021 (Mike1933): the most upvoted method, edit the returned order and add a custom line
  item titled "Corrected Shipping Address - new Mailing Label", shipping required, correct the
  address, buy a label at the right weight, avoiding a zero dollar order. 12 Sep 2023
  (Josh_Zandman): "Just used this method and it worked perfectly." Mike1933 confirms it produces a
  new label and new tracking number, and that the customer sees the custom item rather than their
  products. 27 Aug 2024 (JagXtreme-US): asks "why is this not a standard button" and proposes a
  built-in "Re-delivery after RTS" action.
- Help Center, voiding labels: a label can be voided only if it is not older than 30 days from
  purchase, has no tracking events, was not bought on USPS First Class Mail, and was not added to a
  USPS manifest in the last 15 minutes. Also: "If the package has been shipped, then contact the
  carrier with the package reference number for help." An RTS parcel has tracking events by
  definition, so the void path is closed in practice.
- Shopify Community, 17 Mar 2024 (Fanfi), "How to handle orders Returned to sender?": describes the
  manual routine, mark returned, create a return with no label, restock, then refund or duplicate
  the order at a 100 percent discount to reship free. Reports that Shopify "now deducts returned
  products from daily sales automatically, even if they are not refunded" and marks the order refund
  owed, "which is untrue about 90% of the time". Only reply, 18 Jul 2024 (lindahlcorp): "I wish I
  had a good solution but sadly I don't", and wishes Shopify supported this natively. No staff reply.
- Shopify Community, 29 May 2024 (crastett), "Return to Sender Protocol": "We are getting more and
  more return to sender packages", mostly customer error, customers want a free reship or a full
  refund. Reply 14 Feb 2025 (DaisyVo): refund the product but deduct the original shipping cost
  plus any carrier return fee; customer covers the new shipping on a reship; suggests an RTS
  tracking spreadsheet, which is the manual version of this playbook.
- Shopify Community, 28 Sep 2023 (Jeffro1234), "Advice - Refunding a Return To Sender Order": a 165
  dollar free-shipping order that cost 17.40 to ship, returned after two failed delivery attempts,
  with the customer asking for a refund over two months later. No staff reply.
- Shopify Community feature request, 18 May 2026 (nardevfashion), "Shopify Should Add Separate RTO
  Status Instead of Mixing it with Customer Returns": asks for a separate RTO status, separate RTO
  reporting, RTO APIs and webhooks, and filters in Orders and Analytics, because mixing them
  "creates misleading return-rate data and makes performance analysis inaccurate". Replies the same
  day: order_ops_guy, "An RTO and a customer-initiated return are completely different events
  operationally", reporting "gets noisy and people start making wrong decisions off the same
  dashboard", and manual tags "break down once order volume increases or multiple people touch
  fulfillment". metric_nerd: a brand where "like 60% of it was RTOs from bad pin codes, not actual
  product issues", and splitting the data stopped them discontinuing SKUs that were performing well.
  5 Aug 2026 (LokeshSomaiya69, discloses he builds CODFlip): "RTO isn't a returns problem, it's a
  fulfilment risk problem", and "every app in this space (including ours) has to reconstruct RTO
  signal from fulfilment/tracking events instead of getting a clean, structured status."
- Shopify Community, 28 Apr 2026 (uma0306): an app builder canvassing merchants on RTS losses in
  COD-heavy markets, asking whether the pain is shipping cost loss or manual follow-up, and whether
  merchants keep a manual Excel list of repeat refusers. Zero replies, which is its own data point.

Playbook shipped: read every open shipment's tracking feed hourly for the turnaround scan whatever
wording the carrier uses, flag the order the hour it reverses with a tag and a timeline note, name
the cause as bad address, refused, uncollected or customs and route each differently, email the
customer for a corrected address the same day rather than on day eleven, apply one reship or refund
rule with the second label priced to policy, reconcile and restock on physical arrival rather than
on the scan, and report RTS separately from customer returns, by courier and by region.
