## New post

**Nº 131 - "How to catch Shopify shipping labels you paid for and never used"**
`how-to-catch-shopify-shipping-labels-you-never-used.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-18 - 3,118 words - 10 min read

Pain point: you buy an outbound label, the order gets cancelled, merged, held or reprinted, and the
label stays paid for. Shopify charges for outbound labels at purchase rather than at the carrier
scan, gives you 30 days to void an unused one, returns the money as label credit rather than cash,
and never counts the days. There is no unused label filter, no age column, no Flow trigger for a
shipping label and no void mutation in the Admin API.

Why this topic: high commercial pain, almost nothing written. Everything on the open web about
Shopify label voiding is a how-to-click-the-button walkthrough from an app blog. Nobody has written
about the outbound versus return label billing asymmetry as the reason merchant intuition is wrong
here, nobody has pointed out that the reassuring sentence "you aren't charged for labels that you
don't use" is the second half of a sentence about return labels, nobody has connected the
`cancellable` boolean on the ShippingLabel object to the bulk void limit of 250 as a workable
detection design, and nobody has noted that the only Flow trigger that could watch for a missing
scan is documented as not firing on manual fulfillment events, which is exactly what buying a label
in the admin is. Searchers arrive on "void shopify shipping label" and "shopify unused shipping
label refund" with money on the line.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, Changing and voiding shipping labels: a label can be voided only if it is not older
  than 30 days from the purchase date, has no tracking events, was not bought with USPS First Class
  Mail, and was not added to a USPS shipping manifest in the last 15 minutes. "After you void a
  shipping label, the cost is credited to your account." Labels with tracking events cannot be
  voided and the instruction is to contact the carrier with the package reference number. "You can
  void a maximum of 250 shipping labels at the same time." Tip block, in full: "Return labels can't
  be voided, but they do expire. You aren't charged for labels that you don't use." The Shipping
  labels page shows 100 orders per page and groups purchases into batches by purchase date. For a
  label that cannot be voided the page points to "Buying a replacement label when a label can't be
  voided."
- Help Center, Billing for shipping labels: labels are billed at a spending threshold and once
  reached "you're invoiced and charged immediately"; purchases continue to 110 percent of the
  threshold, worked example 400 dollars allowing 440; if the threshold is not reached in a cycle the
  charges go on the monthly subscription bill; the first label is billed on its own. "Any refunds
  issued for shipping labels are added as a shipping label credit to your account." "Credits are
  applied to future shipping label purchases." "If you void a shipping label before using it, then
  the refund is issued as a credit to your account." Voiding only "within 30 days of purchase."
- Help Center, Buying Shopify Shipping return labels: "Return labels can't be voided, but they can
  expire." USPS return labels expire 1 year after purchase, FedEx 90 days, UPS 6 months. "If a
  return label expires unused, then you aren't charged." "Instead, you're charged only after the
  carrier scans the package." Carriers are USPS, FedEx and UPS.
- Help Center, Canceling orders: "If you purchased a shipping label for this order, then you can
  void the label." Before deleting: "If you purchased a shipping label for the order but didn't use
  it, then void the label before deleting the order." "The Restock inventory option is selected by
  default." Bulk cancellation caps at 250 orders at one time. Partially fulfilled orders cannot be
  cancelled until the fulfillment is cancelled.
- Shopify Flow trigger reference: 83 triggers listed. None relates to a shipping label. Present and
  relevant: Fulfillment created, Fulfillment event created, Order canceled, Fulfillment order
  canceled, Refund created, Scheduled time.
- Shopify Flow, Fulfillment event created: "starts a workflow when changes occur in an existing
  order's fulfillment status", for third-party fulfillment services, with updates such as picked up,
  in transit or delivered, and "Manual fulfillment events do not start workflows with the
  Fulfillment event created trigger."
- Admin GraphQL `shippingLabel` query: "The optional shipping label for this fulfillment." Six
  fields only: `cancellable` ("Indicates whether the label is cancellable or not."), `id`,
  `location`, `printed` ("Indicates whether the label was printed or not."), `shippingDocuments`,
  `trackingInfo`. No purchase date, no cost, no carrier at top level.
- Admin GraphQL `shippingLabelPurchase`: announced in the developer changelog on 17 June 2026 for
  API version 2026-07; requires `write_orders`, a fulfillment order write scope, the
  `buy_shipping_labels` user permission and accepted Shopify Shipping terms; result polls
  PENDING_PURCHASE to PURCHASED or PURCHASE_FAILED; FedEx not supported. No void or refund sibling
  mutation exists or is referenced on either page.
- Shopify Community, 12 June 2026 (Cordan_Fox), "Request: Expose shipping label cost per order via
  Admin GraphQL API": "the cost a merchant pays for a Shopify Shipping label is visible in the order
  timeline" but "it is not accessible via the Admin GraphQL API"; "The Order.transactions,
  Fulfillment, and FulfillmentOrder objects contain no label cost fields."; the only workaround is
  `shopifyPaymentsAccount.balanceTransactions`, which requires Shopify Payments. No staff reply.
- Admin GraphQL `FulfillmentEventStatus`: eleven values, including LABEL_PURCHASED, "A shipping
  label has been purchased.", LABEL_PRINTED, "A purchased shipping label has been printed.", and
  CONFIRMED, "The fulfillment is confirmed. This is the default value when no other information is
  available."
- Shopify Community, 14 July 2022 (Prewarandmore), "Void shipping label after canceling order": "I
  refunded and canceled his order but when I try to void my shipping label, there is no option that
  I can find." 25 July 2022, same poster: "I guess it's not possible?" One non-staff reply in
  September 2022 with a documentation link. Never resolved.
- Shopify Community, 12 September 2022 (KRuss), "Canceling a Shipping Label after item is marked as
  Fulfilled": bought a UPS label, realized the Labor Day holiday would make delivery late, built a
  new shipment and label, asked for a refund on the unused one. Imogen_1, Shopify: label refunds
  cannot be requested through the admin, void for account credit toward future label purchases, and
  for a cash refund contact the Live Team.
- Shopify Community, "Cancelled a shipping label but Shopify isn't giving me the shipping credit":
  11 January 2023 (Mermeladazul) believed she had paid twice for a voided, unused label. Kimi_2
  restated the rules, "Only labels that haven't been used (ie. scanned by the shipping carrier) are
  able to be voided", and that the credit "will be automatically applied towards any shipping label
  purchase with any carrier." 8 March 2024 (Bottleroller) voided two labels, received credit for
  one, and could not find credit information under Billing; Sophia pointed to Settings then Billing
  for the current cycle and otherwise the Help Center. No resolution in the thread.

Playbook shipped: register every label the hour it is bought with order, carrier, service, cost read
from the timeline and day zero; watch only for the first carrier scan; flag the break on cancelled,
refunded, held, replaced or simply never scanned; rank the queue by remaining days rather than
value; read `cancellable` before queueing and drop USPS First Class Mail and anything already
scanned into a carrier claim list; hand over one batch a day sized to the 250 label bulk void limit;
reconcile the credits that appear against the voids performed. Honest limits stated in the post:
there is no void mutation so the final press stays human, label cost needs Shopify Payments balance
transactions or timeline parsing, and an already scanned label leaves this process for a claim.
