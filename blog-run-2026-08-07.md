**Dispatch Nº 102 - "The package that says delivered but never arrived"**
`how-to-handle-delivered-but-not-received-claims-on-shopify.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-07 · 2,540 words · 10 min

Topic: delivered but not received (DNR) claims, the Tuesday email that says the tracking is
wrong and the porch is empty. The post covers the four stories one delivered scan supports
(premature GPS scan, wrong-door drop, porch theft, refund farming) and why each needs a
different answer; the carrier evidence almost nobody pulls (FedEx Picture Proof of Delivery on
no-signature US/Canada residential deliveries, free and login-free on the tracking page; UPS
delivery photos; the USPS GPS readback available at the local Post Office counter but not the
800 number; the Missing Mail search that opens at 7 business days); the 24-48 hour
premature-scan window and the same-hour reply that buys it honestly; the three Shopify
boundaries (no claim object in the admin, Shopify Protect covering only fraudulent and
unrecognized chargebacks so item-not-received is excluded even on protected orders, and Flow's
Fulfillment event created trigger firing only on third-party fulfillment updates with the doc
line "manual fulfillment events do not start workflows"); the four-door decision (wait,
reship, refund, deny) with a thresholds-by-value policy example; and the playbook (hear the
claim on any channel, pull proof in the first hour, run the 48 hour play, score value and
history, decide by policy, execute with signature-required reships and pre-drafted chargeback
responses, report the addresses that keep coming back).
