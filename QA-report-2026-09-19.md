## Why this topic

Checked against all 71 existing posts: no coverage of destination-based product restriction.
Demand is high and persistent (five separate community threads spanning 2021 to 2026, plus
paid apps built solely for it), and the existing content is almost entirely app landing pages
rather than a real explanation of why the platform cannot do it. The pain is also legal rather
than merely operational, which raises intent.

The angle nobody writes up: shipping profiles are the official answer, and they break twice in
a mixed cart. Either the buyer is charged two shipping rates (documented Shopify behaviour,
rates are added together across profiles), or the whole checkout fails and the legal half of
the basket goes down with the restricted half.
