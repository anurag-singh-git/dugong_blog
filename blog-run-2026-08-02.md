## New post

**Dispatch Nº 98 - "The cancellation request that ships anyway"**
`how-to-let-customers-cancel-their-own-orders-on-shopify.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-02 · 2,304 words

Topic: order cancellation requests that lose the race against fulfillment. Shopify added
self-serve cancellation requests on June 18, 2026, one day before the EU's withdrawal-function
requirement (Directive 2023/2673) took effect on June 19, 2026, and the feature is six weeks
old with almost no editorial coverage. The post covers the full arc: the customer who requests
cancellation seventeen minutes after checkout and still receives the parcel; the help-center
fine print (a pending request does not change the order's state, does not pause fulfillment,
does not work with legacy customer accounts, and notifications are email-only with nothing in
the Shopify app); why 3PL feeds, auto-fulfillment, print-on-demand sync, and bulk label
sessions ship canceled orders anyway; the channels problem (most cancellation intent arrives
by email reply, chat, and DM, not through the button); the chargeback and EU-compliance cost of
losing the race; and the failed workarounds (inbox vigilance, blanket 24-hour fulfillment
delays, cancel-button apps, all-sales-final policies). The playbook: match cancellation intent
on every channel to its order, hold fulfillment in seconds before any decision, check the
policy gates, cancel clean cases end to end with refund, restock, tag, and reply, offer the
edit when the customer really wants a change, escalate picked and shipped orders with context,
open the return path honestly when it is too late, and report weekly on causes.
