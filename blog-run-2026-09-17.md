**Dispatch Nº 117 - "The SKU two products share"**
`how-to-fix-duplicate-skus-on-shopify.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-09-17 · 2,499 words (stated 2,500) · 10 min

Topic: duplicate SKUs on Shopify. Inventory in Shopify does not live on the SKU, it lives on an
inventory item, one per variant, with the SKU as a text field beside it, so two variants carrying
the same code are two independent stock counts wearing one name tag. The post covers Shopify's own
documentation (SKUs "must be unique and no two product variants should include the same SKU";
"duplicate SKUs can cause issues with third-party integrations and inventory tracking"; "if a
duplicate SKU is detected, then a warning message displays"; SKUs are case-sensitive, so "ABC123"
and "abc123" are different codes; Shopify Fulfillment Network is the one place unique SKUs are
required), the community record (the August 7, 2019 thread still collecting replies in November
2022, "Unfortunately Shopify doesn't keep in sync inventory for products that have the same SKU"
and "I dont understand why they would even allow duplicate a product if you cant sync the
inventory", answered by merchants and app links rather than staff, at $5, $14 and $29 a month;
the May 24, 2024 fulfillment-service thread showing the problem is structural, one available
number requested per SKU against a different committed quantity subtracted per duplicate variant,
so no single number can be right for both at once, re-asked May 5, 2025, unanswered; and the
September 15, 2026 developer-forum bug, "Even if you tell it to NOT duplicate the SKU, it does
so", filed two days before this dispatch and still open), where duplicates come from (the
Duplicate button, CSV imports matched by handle not SKU, membership and wholesale pricing apps
that clone products for tier pricing, merge files, a custom metafield named SKU colliding on
import, plus the two deliberate cases, bundles/multipacks and blank SKUs), the three costs (split
counts and phantom stock; the pick that becomes a guess once a code reaches a 3PL or scanner,
with Cin7 Core's rules as the contrast, duplicates once refused outright, now accepted behind a
setting with "only make stock adjustments for MainProducts" and 1-1 location mapping; reporting
and feeds that quietly add two products together), the compounding bill (a duplicate found in
week one is a rename, one found in year two has order history attached), the absence of any
duplicate-SKU report in the admin, and the playbook, with the honest limits stated: the tool
proposes renames on products with history and a human approves, and it will not decide which of
two listings is the real product.
