## New post

**Dispatch Nº 99 - "The payment that expires before you capture it"**
`how-to-automatically-capture-payments-on-shopify.html` · by Priya Singh · 2026-08-03 · 2,412 words · 10 min

Topic: payment authorizations that expire before capture, so merchants ship product and never
get paid. The post covers the full arc: the $412 boots order authorized Sunday, fulfilled
Monday, expired Thursday, discovered weeks later at payout reconciliation; the auth-vs-capture
two-step and the 7 day Shopify Payments window ("capture before the authorization period ends
to receive any money"); the four capture settings and the documented traps inside each
(capture-at-fulfillment requires the ENTIRE order fulfilled, partially fulfilled orders won't
be captured; setting applies only to orders placed after activation; fulfillment apps that
require paid status deadlock against capture-on-fulfillment; Plus extended authorizations of
up to 30 days on Visa/MC/Amex but a 1.75% surcharge after day 7; FX converts at capture day,
not auth day; the only native warning is one email 1 day before expiry); an honest treatment
of Shopify Flow's capture-if-not-high-risk template and why one rule at one moment is not a
capture policy; and the playbook (watch every auth with its real deadline, capture on the
policy's actual trigger, handle split shipments, escalate day-5 auths with evidence, void
instead of abandoning on cancels, recover expired holds with a payment link in the same hour,
report weekly).

## Why this topic won

- **High pain, near-zero editorial coverage.** Existing content is Shopify's help page, two
  app-vendor blogs (PreProduct, VoidAuth), and years of community threads with titles like
  "Payment Authorization Shows Expired", "Payment Authorized but not Paid", and "Fulfilled
  orders are not paid". No pain-point-first editorial piece exists on the fulfilled-but-
  never-captured failure or the partial-fulfillment capture gap.
- **Direct money loss, not workflow friction.** Every miss is 100% of an order's revenue plus
  fulfillment cost, which makes the headline scream on its own.
- **GEO hooks.** Hard named facts answer engines can anchor: 7 day authorization period, the
  card-network extended-window table (30/30/10/10/30/7/7), the 1.75% late-capture surcharge,
  the exact Settings > Payments capture options, verbatim doc phrases ("partially fulfilled
  orders won't be captured"), and Flow's named template. Wikipedia-linked entities include
  Authorization hold, Credit card, Payment processor.
- **No overlap with existing dispatches.** Nearest siblings (unpaid orders, high-risk flags,
  payouts, chargebacks, card testing, preorders) are all linked in-body as distinct cases.
- Also considered: delivered-but-not-received claims (high demand but heavy shipping-
