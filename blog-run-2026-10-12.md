## New post

**Nº 125 - "The stock Shopify won't let you sell"**
`how-to-fix-shopify-committed-inventory-that-never-clears.html` · Priya Singh · 2026-10-12 · 2,166 words · 8 min

Pain point: on hand is right, available is zero, and the difference is sitting in committed for
orders that shipped weeks ago. The storefront sells available, the warehouse counts on hand, and
nobody reconciles the gap, so a product the merchant can physically pick off a shelf reads as sold
out. Committed is the one inventory state Shopify will not let anyone adjust, move or query by
order, so every obvious fix is unavailable and the only levers are the orders behind it.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, understanding inventory states: committed is "the number of units that are set aside
  and can't be sold", created by unfulfilled order line items, draft order reserves, and transfers
  marked ready to ship. On hand is editable, committed is not mentioned as editable anywhere.
- Shopify dev, manage quantity states: the valid quantity names are incoming, on_hand, available,
  committed, reserved, damaged, safety_stock, quality_control. On hand equals the sum of available,
  committed, reserved, damaged, safety stock and quality control. In Shopify's own words, "You
  can't use the GraphQL Admin API to adjust or move inventory quantities in the committed state."
  Available, reserved, damaged, safety stock and quality control are all writable.
- Shopify Developer Community, 16 May 2025, "Ability to get breakdown of orders holding committed
  inventory count" (Andrew_Jaanuu): the admin's per-order popover uses a non-public field,
  activeLedgerDocuments. He asks "is there any way to accomplish the same thing with public shopify
  graphql?" Shopify's answer is an approximation via an orders filter on fulfillment_status
  unfulfilled and status open. Tiago_Ramalho hits the same wall for safety stock on 4 Sept 2025:
  ledgerDocumentQuantities, externalQuantityByApp and reservedMovementQuantities are all absent
  from the public schema, and Shopify replies "Currently you can only narrow this down to the name."
  Thread still open.
- Shopify Community, 31 Jan 2024, "Committed inventory showing fulfilled order" (Theaj): "i
  fulfilled the order but committed inventory still showing that order." Three more merchants
  confirm, one with a 16-item order showing 18 committed, one across multiple products, one saying
  "We have to wait, I guess." Last post Oct 2024 still asking for an update. No staff reply.
- Shopify Community, 8 Feb 2023, "Committed inventory is negative" (Priv_Acy): committed at -40 on
  80 on hand. Staff (Shay) confirms "Your Committed quantity shouldn't be showing negative values",
  calls it a known issue. Merchant reports "our inventory now shows HUNDREDS more items than are
  actually available." A later reply traces it to adjustments labelled "data correction" that reset
  committed to zero, after which fulfilling older orders drove it negative.
- Shopify Community, 26 Aug 2020, "Cannot de-stock item from location because of unfulfilled order"
  (moorka): "Can't unstock from this location because it has unfulfilled orders" on a cancelled test
  order. Same report Dec 2020 and Jan 2021. Workaround posted Apr 2024 (JoesIdeas): "Committed
  inventory just means it's assigned to that location in an order" and "you have to either
  re-assign the location or fulfill the item to clear that committed inventory." Never confirmed by
  the OP. Five years, no native fix.
- Help Center, creating draft orders: reserving puts the units "in the Committed inventory state and
  can't be purchased by other customers", and the merchant picks the expiry date themselves. The
  page says nothing about what happens when a reservation expires.
- Shopify Community, 4 Dec 2025, "Store won't show items as sold out with multiple locations when
  items are commited": with fulfillable inventory on, stock that is committed but unshipped keeps
  the product visible, and the customer is rejected at checkout for their country. Reply: "if you
  can't fix committed inventory you need advanced theme custom." Unresolved.
- Shopify Developer Community, 24 Oct 2025: Storefront API quantityAvailable returned one global
  total for every @inContext country, so a US location at -100 masked 69 real units in AU.
  Reproduced by Shopify, fix released 8 Jan 2026, contextual values require "Show only fulfillable
  inventory" enabled.
- Shopify Community, 2 Aug 2025, "InventoryLevels.committed help": Flow cannot read committed
  natively; the working answer is quantities(names: ["incoming", "committed"]) through a send admin
  API request or third-party GraphQL action, compared in a run code step.

Why it won: high demand with almost nothing written for it. Page one for "committed inventory
shopify how to fix" is unresolved forum threads plus a Square community thread. No vendor
editorial, no Shopify guide, no merchant-level article anywhere. Threads run continuously from 2020
