## New post

**Nº 128 - "How to stop reordering Shopify stock you already have on order"**
`how-to-stop-reordering-shopify-stock-you-already-have-on-order.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-15 - 2,341 words - 8 min

Pain point: incoming inventory counts units in transit, not units you have bought. A purchase
order the supplier has not shipped contributes nothing, available excludes incoming anyway, so the
low stock report puts a SKU back on the buy list that is already on order, and somebody buys the
same pallet twice. The other half of the same gap is that nothing watches a purchase order date,
so a late delivery is discovered when the shelf empties.

Why this topic: high commercial pain, very thin content on the open web. Everything written about
Shopify purchase orders is either Stocky-sunset app marketing or reorder-point theory. Nobody has
written about the incoming-versus-on-order distinction, and nobody has written about the purchase
order API at all, because it only reached GA two weeks ago.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, inventory states: "Incoming inventory refers to inventory that's on its way to your
  location from inventory transfers or apps." Units not received are excluded from On hand and
  cannot be sold. Available "excludes Committed, Unavailable, and Incoming inventory." The page
  names purchase orders only in the Incoming section and says clicking a variant's incoming
  quantity shows which transfers, purchase orders or apps make up the number.
- Shopify Community, 2 Oct 2026: merchant testing on a development store in September reports "the
  number shown as incoming on a product is narrower than it looks" and "Incoming counts units that
  are in transit." Transfers in draft add nothing; transfers ready to ship but not dispatched add
  nothing; a PO the supplier has not shipped shows zero. No staff reply.
- Shopify Community, 24 Jul 2026 (HectorGomez), "Purchase Order - Incoming Inventory not working":
  incoming stopped reflecting open POs after the new PO system launched. "my reports keep telling
  me to procure what is already in process." Asks for a revert, flags capital management risk. No
  staff reply. 30 minutes later he posts his own workaround: create a transfer from the PO and mark
  it in transit before it has shipped, which he calls frustrating. Reply from lumine the same
  evening: incoming derives from transfers and shipments, not POs; as of 2026-07 the Admin GraphQL
  API had InventoryTransfer and InventoryShipment and no PurchaseOrder; editing the PO or receiving
  a short shipment will not update the transfer, which "can silently re-create overcounting";
  suggests storing the PO number in a transfer metafield so reports can join the records. This
  thread is the spine of the post.
- Shopify Community, 16 Sep 2026 (joeria), "Purchase Orders and Transfers": wants the linked
  transfer to update when products are added to the PO. "The current workflow requires duplicate
  manual edits." Reply quotes Shopify documentation: once a PO and an inventory transfer are
  linked, "they operate independently." No staff reply.
- Shopify Community, 8 Apr 2024 (HectorGomez), "Stocky purchase orders not syncing incoming
  inventory". 16 Apr 2024 (RDS13): five active or partially received POs, Stocky "completely
  unaware of these incoming inventories", low stock report inaccurate, support told them to
  re-enter the data manually. 9 Jul 2024 (afauth): has anyone resolved it. No staff reply. Two
  years, two systems, same hole.
- Shopify Community, Feb to Jul 2026, "API endpoint coming soon for Purchase Orders, with Stocky
  sunset?": Gabe_Stillwater reports support's only suggestion was paying for an ERP; Philip_Gangi,
  May 2026, "The Native PO system is fundamentally incomplete"; muttsonline shares a support table
  listing min/max replenishment as "Not planned for 2026" and bulk CSV import as "Not currently
  available"; Philip_Rose, 8 Jul 2026, "there's still no Purchase Order API". No staff replies.
  Stocky shut down 31 Aug 2026 (confirmed across multiple vendor and agency sources).
- Shopify Community, 26 Jan 2026 (BDEDDY), "Shopfy Flow Limitations - Incoming Inventory & Purchase
  Orders": quotes Sidekick, "Shopify Flow cannot access 'incoming' inventory quantities for
  reporting and automation." Revived 5 Oct 2026 by Taha_Dev and Farzam_saadat, both confirming
  nothing has changed and recommending a custom app or a scheduled Admin API job. No staff reply.
- Flow trigger reference cross-checked. Inventory bench is seven triggers: inventory item created,
  inventory item deleted, inventory transfer completed, inventory transfer ready to ship, product
  variant back in stock, product variant out of stock, product variant inventory quantity changed.
  No purchase order trigger, no incoming inventory trigger, nothing that fires on a date passing.
- Shopify Developer Community, 11 to 14 Aug 2026 (Howard_Lee), "Read_inventory_purchase_orders
  access scope DENIED": ACCESS_DENIED on inventoryPurchaseOrders, scope absent from the public
  access scopes reference and the Dev Dashboard selector. Wes-Dev-Shopify, 12 Aug: declaring it in
  shopify.app.toml passes validation but deploy fails with an app_access error. 14 Aug: server-side
  fix, read_inventory now sufficient on preview stores.
- Shopify Developer Community, 29 Sep 2026 (api-builder-jp): inventoryPurchaseOrders absent in
  2026-07, present in 2026-10. Staff reply KyleG-Shopify, 2 Oct 2026: 2026-10 calls need
  read_inventory_purchase_orders and the scope "is available to all apps now", outside the preview.
  Five of the poster's six follow-up questions went unanswered.
- Admin GraphQL, InventoryPurchaseOrder: "A purchase order represents an agreement from a merchant
  to buy goods or services from a supplier." Fields: name, status, currency, dateCreated,
  orderedAt, archivedAt, destination (LocationSnapshot), origin (InventorySupplierSnapshot),
  lineItems (searchable by unit cost, variant id, supplier SKU, incoming quantity), transfers.
- Admin GraphQL, InventoryPurchaseOrderStatus: exactly two values. DRAFT, "Purchase order is open
  and not marked as ordered yet." ORDERED, "Purchase order is open and marked as ordered." No
  received, no partially received, no closed, no late. No expected arrival field on the object.
- Shopify Developer Community feature requests, unresolved: 16 Jun 2026 (Colby) asking for read,
  create and receive, noting transfer and shipment types do not cover supplier or PO level
  tracking; 30 Jul 2026 asking for a firm answer on a twelve month horizon.

Playbook shipped: pull every ordered-and-not-fully-received PO, reconcile receipts against the
linked transfers, date it from supplier lead time, flag late in days, publish a true on-order
quantity per variant beside available, block the duplicate at draft time, chase the supplier with
the open lines, score lead time actual versus promised and short ship rate.
