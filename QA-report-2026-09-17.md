## Defects found and fixed

### 1-2. Two broken links in the brand-new Nº 117 (content, high)

The generator line-wrapped two `href` values *inside the attribute*, so the URLs carried
literal newlines and eight spaces:

```
<a href="how-
        to-sync-bundle-inventory-with-component-stock-on-
        shopify.html">
```

Browsers strip the newline but percent-encode the spaces, so this resolved to
`how-%20%20%20%20%20%20%20%20to-sync-...`, a guaranteed 404. Verified by resolving the
markup in a live browser. Both hrefs collapsed to single-line URLs; the visible text and
source wrapping are untouched. A site-wide scan found no other whitespace inside any `href`
or `src`, so this is new with Nº 117 and worth a look in the generator's link writer.

### 3-6. Four WCAG AA contrast failures (design/accessibility)

Every text/background pair on the site was measured against its true composited background.
Four small-text combinations failed the 4.5:1 minimum. All four are palette tokens, and each
was darkened along its own hue so the design reads identically:

| Element | Token | Before | After | Contrast before → after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `.tag-essay` pill (75 places) | `--green` | `#0a8f54` | `#0b6f41` | 3.31 → **5.00** |
| `.tag-postmortem` pill + `.callout-sand` marks (~200 places) | `--pink` | `#c61f93` | `#ae1b81` | 4.12 → **5.06** |
| `.byline-avatar` "PS", white on gold (71 posts) | `--gold` | `#b5882c` | `#8d6a22` | 3.22 → **4.98** |
| `.byline-avatar` "AR", white on terracotta (index) | inline hex | `#c47a55` | `#a35c38` | 3.35 → **5.07** |

Ratios are worst-case across every background these appear on (cream `#f6f6f1`, white cards,
and `--surface-2`). The `rgba()` tint backgrounds and borders were deliberately left at the
original hues, so the pills look the same, only the text is legible. Applied to all 72 pages
that carry the palette; the two variant stylesheets remain byte-identical within their groups,
so the count of distinct stylesheets is still exactly 2.

Also changed on the index: the hero badge **"NEW THIS WEEK" → "LATEST DISPATCH"**. The old
copy is a standing lie whenever a publish slips, as it had for 19 days. The new wording is
accurate on every possible publish cadence. The generator's own ticker copy has already moved
to the evergreen "◆ RECENT DISPATCHES", so no change was needed there.

## Content and readability

- **Prose is clean.** Across every article body: no doubled words, no misspellings from the
  common-error list, no stray punctuation spacing, no template leftovers, no `undefined`/`NaN`,
  no double-escaped entities. The only six "doubled word" hits are the artifact of a headline
  ending in "Shopify" running into a paragraph starting with "Shopify", not defects.
- **Line length is right.** Article column measures 624px at desktop, roughly 60 characters per
  line, inside the 50-75 band that reads best.
- **Type is comfortable.** Body 17px at 1.6 line-height on the index, 18.4px in article bodies.
  Exactly one declaration site-wide, so nothing drifts.
- Every `<img>` on the site carries an `alt` attribute.

## Verification method note

All counts recomputed from the files after the publish settled; nothing carried forward.
Card-date comparison uses the site's unpadded-day convention (SEP 9, not SEP 09). Contrast was
computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula against backgrounds composited through every
translucent layer, cross-checked in a live browser against the real rendered DOM. Broken-link
behaviour was confirmed by resolving the actual markup in a browser rather than inferred.
Every fix was re-verified by re-running the full audit from scratch on the final tree.
