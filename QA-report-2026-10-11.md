# QA report - 2026-10-11

Scheduled autonomous run. Full-tree QA of blog.dugong.live. The daily blog run (Dispatch Nº 124)
had finished writing three minutes before this QA started; everything below was measured against
the settled tree of 158 HTML files (80 rendered, 78 stubs) plus feed.xml, sitemap.xml, llms.txt
and llms-full.txt. Five defects found and fixed. No facts, FAQ answers or headlines changed, and
no new marketing copy was written: every text change either normalises a spelling or reuses copy
already published on the same page.

## Method

- Structural audit of all 158 HTML files: tag and CSS brace balance, one h1 per rendered page,
  JSON-LD parses and field completeness, internal links, in-page anchors, stylesheet/script/image
  references, canonical vs og:url, html lang, viewport, charset, meta description length and
  uniqueness, title length and uniqueness, duplicate ids, replacement characters and mojibake,
  unterminated entities, doubled words measured inside single text nodes, heading-level skips,
  stub noindex/canonical, visible date vs JSON-LD datePublished, dispatch-number collisions.
- Headless Chromium render of all 80 rendered pages at 320, 360, 375, 414, 768, 1024, 1280 and
  1920 px (640 renders per pass, three passes): horizontal page overflow, elements escaping the
  viewport, computed font size of every text-bearing element, broken images, tap-target height at
  phone widths, console and page errors.
- WCAG AA contrast on every text node of all 80 pages at 375 px and 1280 px, each measured against
  its resolved composited background with alpha blending through ancestors.
- Word count of every article body (`.article-body`) against the figure the page states in its
  ticker, its article meta and its BlogPosting `wordCount`.
- Coverage cross-check: sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt against the file tree.
- Link graph: inbound and outbound internal links per page, stub redirect resolution.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **twitter:description was stale on 46 of 80 pages.** The site's convention is that the meta
   description, og:description and twitter:description carry the same text; 34 pages did and 46 did
   not, because an earlier pass rewrote the description and og:description but never touched the
   Twitter tag. Nine of the stale values ran past the ~200 character limit X renders, so the card
   text truncated mid-sentence: worst were 291 characters on the purchase-limit post, 284 on the
   free-gift post, and 262, 261, 229, 228, 225, 221 and 209 on seven others. All 46 now carry the
   page's own og:description verbatim. Nothing new was written.

2. **og:description ran past the 165-character ceiling on three pages.** The automations field
   study (200), the WISMO post (181) and the address-validation post (182). On each, og:description
   and twitter:description now carry that page's own meta description (159, 158, 159).

3. **404.html had no Twitter card at all** and its meta description was 166 characters, one over
   the site ceiling. Added twitter:card, twitter:title, twitter:description and twitter:image to
   match the other 79 rendered pages, and trimmed the description to 161 by dropping one word
   ("the full archive" to "the archive").

4. **UK spellings in a US-English tree.** 34 occurrences across 6 pages plus feed.xml, llms.txt and
   llms-full.txt: grey (11), fulfilment (4), colour (2), defence (2), apologise (2), programme (1),
   labelled (3, against 11 existing "labeled"), and their mirrored copies in the llms files and the
   feed. All normalised. Heaviest file was the new Nº 124 returns post (7). "analyses" and
   "cancelled" were left alone: the first is correct US English, the second is the site's dominant
   form at 192 uses against 44 of "canceled".

5. **Sub-12px text throughout, on every page, at phone widths.** 22 distinct selectors rendered
   between 10.24 px and 11.52 px at 320 to 560 px, below the 12 px floor Lighthouse treats as
   legible: tag pills at 10.24, byline avatars, callout marks and CTA fine print at 10.88, card
   dates and authors at 11.20, post meta, read time, footer headings, stat labels and the copyright
   line at 11.52, plus the homepage art coordinates (10.40), archive dates (10.56), signup label
   and fine print, and author role. The previous run's block had lifted only the ticker. A new
   mobile-only block (marked "QA 2026-10-11", ≤560 px, with the nav CTA handled at ≤360 px) puts a
   12 px floor under all of them. Desktop is untouched, and the tag pills' letter-spacing is held
   at 0.05em in the same rule so the pills do not grow wider as the type grows. Applied to all 81
   files that carry a stylesheet. Re-rendered at all eight widths: still zero horizontal overflow.
