## New post

**Nº 141 - "How to fix Shopify orders with no phone number before the label fails"**
`how-to-fix-shopify-orders-with-no-phone-number-before-the-label-fails.html` - Priya Singh -
2026-11-16 - 2,194 words - 8 min read

Pain point: an order arrives with no phone number on the shipping address, and either the label
refuses to buy or it prints without the number and the parcel stalls in the destination country.
Shopify's checkout phone field has three states, do not include, optional and required, and the
setting applies to every country and market the store sells to. So the merchant chooses between
taxing every domestic checkout with a field it does not need and accepting that a share of
international orders will arrive unlabelable. Almost everyone picks the second, correctly, and then
builds nothing to cover it.

Why this topic: high demand, almost no written mechanism, and the search results for the symptom are
app listings and generic label troubleshooting. Nobody has put together that the setting is global
by design rather than by oversight; that the per country version has been asked for since at least
2023 and still needs a Plus checkout app; that the failure has a loud mode and a silent mode and the
silent one costs more; that the pickup point flow skips the very form the required setting governs,
so the setting cannot help there at all and the order cannot be edited afterwards; that a Shopify
order carries three separate phone fields while the label reads exactly one, which means a large
share of these are recoverable without contacting anyone; and that Flow can detect the gap but can
do nothing after the flag.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Shopify Help Center, Editing the checkout form options: shipping address phone number is
  "Don't include", "Optional" (the default on new stores) or "Required", the last described as
  for when "your payment provider or shipping carrier needs a phone number"; customer information
  settings "apply to every country and market that you sell to"; varying fields by country "you
  need an app" and those apps are Shopify Plus only; Required is only enforced when checkout
  includes a shipping address step.
- Shopify Community, "Is it possible to make a phone number field mandatory for some countries and
  not others in Checkout": a May 2023 reply gives steps for a country setting under Form options,
  and Dugen, Nice_Meghan and fullstackdesign each report it is not in their admin, the last in
  October 2024. JohnE10, June 2026, "the best and only way (as far as I could find) is to use an
  app", with a disclosed interest. A second thread asks for the same thing in its title.
- ShippyPro, Shopify shipping label errors: "Could not create label" (030009) is a carrier
  validation error usually from FedEx, most commonly triggered by a missing or malformed phone
  number, with country code and destination digit count both mattering; address line limits of 35
  characters for DHL and UPS and 32 for Evri; blank first or last name rejected by FedEx and
  others; some carriers require a valid phone on the origin address under Settings and Locations.
- Shopify Community, "International Shipping Labels: SHOPIFY CAUSING THOUSANDS in Loss Due to
  Missing Info": phone number absent from the label, parcels reaching the destination country and
  being held by the post office, about 20 orders a month, reships at the merchant's cost, a
  comparison against another provider's label that carried the number, and support dismissing it
  twice. Treated in the post as one store's account of its own carrier mix, not a universal rule.
- Shopify Community, "Critical issue with Mondial Relay pickup point orders, labels blocked due to
  missing phone number": the pickup flow skips the standard shipping address form that the required
  setting governs; Shopify Shipping refuses with "A recipient phone number is required for pickup
  point labels"; pickup point orders cannot be edited, so the number cannot be added later; manual
  portal labels about 5.99 euro against about 3.30 euro on Shopify's negotiated rate, and one
  merchant at about 4.51 euro after migrating a carrier account by hand; as of 30 August 2026 the
  report was only forwarded to Shopify's product team, no fix and no timeline.
- Shopify Community, "Phone number required for pickup delivery": "for pickup point delivery, it
  has become optional, so most customers simply don't enter it"; a cart attribute prefill that
  customers could delete; support spending over an hour and attributing it to the carrier, which
  the merchant disputes because the integration is Shopify's own.
- Shopify Help Center, Conditions in Shopify Flow: the "Empty or does not exist" operator returns
  true for empty or null fields, and conditions reference nested fields, with
  `order.shippingAddress.country` given as the documented example. That is what makes the ten
  minute detection workflow real, and the limits around it are what make it insufficient.
- Atlas Pickup Points, Recommended store settings: tells merchants to set the shipping address
  phone to Required, because "many carriers that deliver to pickup points require the buyer's
  phone number to create a shipping label" and use it to notify the buyer on arrival.
- Order Tagger knowledge base: a documented standard recipe is tagging an order with the billing
  phone when the shipping phone is empty, which is the evidence that the number is routinely
  already on the order, one field across.

Playbook shipped: check each order against what its destination and shipping method actually
require rather than flagging every blank field; recover the number from the billing address, the
customer record and the customer's earlier orders before contacting anyone; ask once, on a channel
the customer reads; write the number onto the shipping address, where the label looks, rather than
leaving it in a support thread; hold the affected order instead of the whole batch; scope a checkout
rule only where the weekly report says it earns its conversion cost; and report misses weekly by
country and shipping method with the recovery rate beside them. Honest limits stated in the post: it
cannot invent a number that exists nowhere on the order, it cannot make a customer reply, and it
cannot edit a pickup point order that Shopify will not let anyone edit.

Overlap handled deliberately: Nº 2 (bad addresses) owns address validity, Nº 127 (HS codes) owns
customs data, and Nº 138 (Flow emails that never send) owns the marketing consent gate that is the
reason Flow cannot ask the customer for the number. This post links to all three in prose and keeps
its weight on the phone field itself, the two failure modes, and the pickup point hole.
