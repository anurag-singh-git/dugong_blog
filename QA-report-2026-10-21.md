# QA report, 2026-10-21

Scope: every rendered page on blog.dugong.live, checked for formatting,
design, mobile friendliness, readability and content accuracy. Pages were
rendered in headless Chromium at 320, 360, 390, 768 and 1280 CSS pixels with
the real Hanken Grotesk, Inter and JetBrains Mono webfonts loaded, not
fallbacks.

Note on timing: this pass started against 99 pages and the daily publishing
run fired into the repository partway through, shipping dispatch Nº 133
(`how-to-bulk-edit-variant-metafields-on-shopify.html`). Editing stopped
until that run went quiet, then every fix below was re-applied over the new
state and the whole site was re-rendered at all five widths. Final count: 100
rendered pages, 87 dispatches. Every fix in this pass is in a re-runnable
script, so the overlap cost nothing.

`_tools/seo-check.py` passed before this pass and passes after it.

## What was wrong, and what was done

### 1. Two pages scrolled sideways on every phone

`how-to-catch-shopify-shipping-label-adjustments-before-they-eat-your-margin.html`
and the new Nº 133 both pushed the document past the viewport at 320, 360 and
390px: 450px and 358px of content in a 320px window, with the paragraph text
clipped on the right.

The cause in both cases was a long dotted API identifier written as ordinary
prose: `shopifyPaymentsAccount.balanceTransactions` sets a 430px unbreakable
word, and `product.metafields.namespace.key` a 338px one. An earlier pass had
given `overflow-wrap: anywhere` to `<code>` inside the article body, but the
dispatches normally quote an API field in plain prose, not in `<code>`, so the
rule never applied where it was needed.

`.article-body p, .article-body li, .article-body td, .article-body dd` now
carry `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on all 99 pages that have an article body. The
rule only breaks a word that cannot otherwise fit, so running prose is
unchanged, and the next GraphQL field name cannot break a phone again.

### 2. The 12px legibility floor was switched off above 560px

The 2026-10-11 pass set a 12px floor for micro-labels and explicitly scoped it
to `@media (max-width: 560px)` with the comment "desktop unchanged". 768px is
not a desktop. Every tablet in portrait was rendering the topic tags at
10.24px, the card dates and authors at 11.2px and the read-time labels at
11.52px: 15,000 elements of sub-12px text across the site, on the same touch
devices the floor was written for.

The floor now runs to 1024px. Sub-12px text at 768px and below: 15,000 before,
zero after. Above 1024px the typography is exactly as designed.

The ticker had the same shape of bug from the 2026-10-10 pass, its 12px floor
also scoped to 560px, leaving the marquee at the top of every page at 11px on
every tablet and desktop. Raised to 12px over the same range, with the
letter-spacing that size was tuned with.

### 3. The new dispatch's "Read the ... guide" links named the brand in lowercase

All eight `.qa-guide-link` calls to action on
`shopify-automation-questions.html` read "Read the shopify order management
automation guide". The guide name is interpolated from the slug, so the brand
arrived lowercase in the middle of a sentence that starts with a capital.
Fixed on all eight. The all-lowercase `.hub-more` and `.foot-tag` microcopy is
a deliberate style and was left alone.

### 4. 404.html was five dispatches behind under the heading "Latest dispatches"

The list is hand-written and the daily run does not touch it, so it ended at
2026-10-14 and omitted the five newest dispatches. It is now rebuilt from the
ten newest dispatches by `published_time`, and the rebuild is part of the
re-runnable fix script rather than a hand edit, so it will not drift again
silently.

### 5. Eighteen tap targets under 24px

* The eight `.qa-guide-link` links measured 18px. Their sibling
  `.qa-post-title a` was given a 24px floor on 2026-10-14 and this one was
  missed.
* The ten links in 404.html's dispatch list measured 20px from 561px up. They
  only passed on a phone because the titles wrapped to two lines there.

Both now sit at 24px (WCAG 2.5.8). Tap targets under 24px across the whole
site at all five widths: 41 before, zero after, with one documented exception
below.

### 6. Stale counts and date ranges, again

The daily run updates card lists and most counts and has now left the
"FIRST -> LAST" line and the dispatch count behind on six page-visits across
this one pass, before and after its own run:

| Page | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| shopify-automation-questions.html | 84, then 85 DISPATCHES | 86 |
| shopify-order-management-automation.html | MAY 28 -> SEP 21 | MAY 28 -> OCT 19 |
| shopify-catalog-and-storefront-operations.html | 6 DISPATCHES, JUN 17 -> AUG 25 | 7, JUN 17 -> OCT 21 |
| priya-singh.html | MAY 28 -> OCT 18, then OCT 19 | MAY 28 -> OCT 21 |

`_qa_tmp/run-10-18/fix_stale_meta.py` recomputes all of these from the
dispatches each page actually links. This is the fourth consecutive pass to
fix the same thing, which is an argument for folding that script into the
publishing run rather than the QA pass.

### 7. An unmarked verbatim forum quote read as broken authorial prose

On `how-to-fix-duplicate-skus-on-shopify.html`: "One reply asked the question
everybody in the thread was actually asking: I dont understand why they would
even allow duplicate a product if you cant sync the inventory." Two missing
apostrophes and a grammatical error, with nothing to tell a reader they are
the merchant's and not the writer's. Now quoted, verbatim, in the visible copy
only; the JSON-LD description paraphrases the same passage and a raw quote
mark there would have broken the JSON.

### 8. READ NEXT convention drift on two pages

The 2026-10-18 pass put the first six READ NEXT cards in the grid and the rest
behind a `<details>`. Two dispatches published after that pass carried eight
cards and no disclosure, so they would have kept accruing cards with nothing
to hold them. Both split, and `_qa_tmp/run-10-21/split_related.py` now
enforces the convention on any dispatch page, so it can be re-run after a
publishing run.

### 9. Smaller things

* `behaviour` -> `behavior` on two pages, against 82 that say `behavior`.
  `Personalisation` was left as written: it appears only inside the quoted
  title of a Shopify community thread.
* One curly apostrophe (`we&rsquo;re` on aanya-rao.html) against straight
  quotes on every other page. Normalised.
* `canceled` was left as written in all six places: each one quotes a Shopify
  UI status or Flow trigger name verbatim.

## What was checked and found clean

* No horizontal scroll, no element past the viewport, no clipped text on any
  of 100 pages at 320, 360, 390, 768 or 1280px.
* No sub-12px text at any width up to and including 768px.
* No console error, no page error, no broken image (the site uses no `<img>`).
* All 100 pages parse without an HTML5 error; one `h1` per page, no
  heading-level skips, no duplicate element ids.
* No em or en dashes, no mojibake, no placeholder text, no unbalanced quotes
  or parentheses in any visible copy.
* Spelling: every unknown token on the new dispatch is a GraphQL mutation name
  (`productVariantsBulkUpdate`, `metafieldsSet`, `stagedUploadsCreate`) or
  domain vocabulary. Site-wide, the only non-vocabulary hits are the
  deliberate in-quote misspellings (`Jhon`, `gmial.com`).
* Mean sentence length 18.4 words, median 14, across 28,139 sentences.
* Page height at 390px: median 21,648px, worst 32,302px (priya-singh.html, the
  full author index).
* `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured on every animated page.
* 404.html's list now matches the ten newest dispatches exactly.

## Known, deliberate, left alone

* The byline and hero separator dots run at 3.03:1. The 2026-10-12 pass set
  that deliberately and gave them `aria-hidden="true"`: they are decorative
  glyphs, which is the correct treatment, and 1.4.3 does not apply to them.
  638 instances across five widths, all of them that one dot.
* One inline link, "Shopify Flow" on the gift-card dispatch, measures 21px.
  WCAG 2.5.8 exempts a target whose size is set by the line-height of the
  sentence it sits in.
* Micro-label typography above 1024px is unchanged from the design.
* `132 dispatches published` on the homepage against 87 dispatch pages. This
  tracks the issue number and is consistent with the masthead. Flagged for the
  third pass running: the label says "dispatches published", so the number is
  read as a page count by anyone who counts. Worth a decision.
* The footer's product and company columns still point seven differently
  labelled links at `https://dugong.live`. Fixing that needs the real
  destination URLs on the marketing site, which this pass cannot verify.
