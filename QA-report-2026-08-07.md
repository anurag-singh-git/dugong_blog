## Fix applied

### Owner's index.html mobile-nav fix propagated to all 54 wired posts

Commit `f848267` repaired the sticky nav for small screens on the homepage, but every post
page shares the identical nav component (brand mark + "dugong / field notes" + "Try Dugong
free" CTA) and still carried the pre-fix CSS: fixed `height: 66px`, no flex gap, no
`min-width: 0` on the brand, no `white-space`/`flex-shrink` protection on the CTA, and no
rules below 820px. On ~360-480px viewports the brand plus CTA overflow the fixed-height row,
so the CTA wraps or clips — the exact failure the owner fixed on index. Verified before
patching that all 54 wired posts contained the pre-fix strings byte-identically and had no
alternative small-screen nav handling of their own.

Applied the owner's fix verbatim to each wired post:

- `.nav` gains `gap: 0.75rem` and `height` becomes `min-height: 66px`
- `.brand` gains `min-width: 0`
- `.nav-cta` gains `white-space: nowrap; flex-shrink: 0;`
- new `@media (max-width: 480px)` hides `.brand-slash`/`.brand-section` and shrinks the CTA;
  new `@media (max-width: 360px)` shrinks it further

The 4 wired posts that embed the signup form (best-shopify-automations, handle-high-risk-orders,
prevent-overselling, reduce-wismo-tickets) also received the matching signup fix from the same
commit: `min-width: 0` on the input, `white-space: nowrap` on the button, and a
`@media (max-width: 420px)` rule stacking the row vertically.

Every replacement ran with a strict exactly-one-occurrence assertion per file. Diff arithmetic
confirms surgical scope: 54 files, 618 insertions, 170 deletions = 54×(11 ins, 3 del) for the
nav + 4×(6 ins, 2 del) for signup, all inside `<style>`, zero content lines touched. The two
noindexed rejects were deliberately left unpatched (standing recommendation remains to delete
them, not repair them).
