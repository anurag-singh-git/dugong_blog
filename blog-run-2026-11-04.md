## New post

**Nº 139 - "How to fix Shopify Flow emails that never send"**
`how-to-fix-shopify-flow-emails-that-never-send.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-11-04 -
2,841 words - 10 min read

Pain point: you build a Flow workflow that emails a customer, it runs hundreds of times, and it
delivers almost nothing. Flow ships with exactly one email action, Send marketing email, and its
recipient field is fixed to customers subscribed to email marketing. Everyone else produces an
exception rather than a message, in a run log nobody opens. Most buyers are in that group, because
the checkout opt-in is optional and has to stay optional, and double opt-in holds the ones who do
tick the box in pending until they click a link most of them never click.

Why this topic: high demand, almost no written mechanism. The open web on this keyword is app
listings for transactional-email add-ons and generic posts about Shopify Email. Nobody has put
together that Flow has no transactional email action at all and that this is deliberate rather
than missing; that the gate reports itself as an exception rather than a skip, so a healthy-looking
workflow is the normal symptom; that abandoned checkout is the single marketing automation whose
recipients can be widened; that the Admin API's five states carry a distinction (never asked versus
said no) that no send-time check preserves; that pending is a permanent cost of double opt-in with
no merchant-side way to clear it and Shopify's own advice against reminding people; and that the
consent record in Shopify and the one in the email tool routinely disagree, with the lower of the
two deciding delivery.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Shopify Community, 4 January 2023 (alexpinson), "Sending emails with Shopify automation, send
  regardless of opt-in status": "there's no option to deselect 'To: Customers subscribed to email
  marketing'". Jeff-Armstrong, 5 January: "The only way around this currently is to use a
  third-party app's email action in Flow." paul_n, same day, on why: "Flow cannot guarantee that
  the email would not be used for marketing purposes," with the shared-deliverability argument.
  No post marked as a solution.
- Shopify Community, 16 October 2025 (Abrar1), "Shopify Flow email error, customer not subscribed
  to marketing": the exception text, "Email not sent because {{ customerName }} is not subscribed
  to marketing," against a fulfilment window of about fifty days. tim_tairli, same day: "Since the
  action is called 'Send marketing email' it only suitable to respect the marketing state
  property." Sulyman (Shopify Specialist), 17 October: "there's currently no built-in Flow action
  for transactional emails." ri31, 22 April 2026, on messaging automations: "are sent only to
  customers who are subscribed to marketing." Nothing marked as a solution.
- Shopify Help Center, Create marketing automations: "Marketing automations send only to customers
  subscribed to marketing by default"; abandoned checkout is the one exception, "you can change the
  To: field to All customers in the email editor"; and "You can't override this setting for SMS or
  WhatsApp automations."
- Shopify Help Center, Collecting customer contact information: consent "is captured at checkout
  when they select the marketing checkbox and proceed with checkout"; "When the checkbox is
  preselected, customers can still choose to deselect it"; regions for preselection are a setting,
  and choosing none means it is never preselected; double opt-in "requires customers to click a
  link in an email to confirm"; "Customers who subscribe to your mailing before you activate double
  opt-in aren't required to confirm their status."
- Shopify Developer Docs, CustomerEmailAddressMarketingState: five values, with NOT_SUBSCRIBED as
  "The customer is not subscribed to email marketing" and UNSUBSCRIBED as "The customer is not
  subscribed to email marketing but was previously subscribed," plus PENDING, SUBSCRIBED and
  INVALID.
- Shopify Community, 7 February 2023 onward, "How do I confirm pending customer emails?": Elias
  (Shopify), 8 February, "you don't need to do anything to confirm the subscriptions at this
  stage"; 28 November 2023, "I wouldn't recommend sending a reminder to customers as it could also
  be seen as spam." KD30, 27 December 2023, thousands pending, asking for a bulk change to
  subscribed, no reply. EdMoDecor, 6 and 27 December 2024, 450+ pending then about half of all
  customers, "How can we send a second email to all 'pending' customers?", no reply.
- Shopify Community, 1 September 2023 (TileOutlets), "Email subscriptions, over 200 pending":
  "only a fair few have actually accepted the email marketing email (double opt-in)."
- Shopify Community, 4 August 2023 (Nathanle8), "Customers who ordered and email marketing": asking
  whether checkout can require a subscription, warned that a forced checkbox is not lawful
  everywhere, and told (IshaMehra, 17 June 2025) that Shopify separates customers from email
  subscribers and the opt-in must stay optional.
- Klaviyo Community, 22 September 2024 (queenb), "Accepts marketing in Shopify but not subscribed
  in Klaviyo": accepted answer that accepts marketing is a Shopify field, that the subscription
  depends on the sync being enabled, and that it applies only from the day it is turned on, so
  historical contacts need a manual update. evilesther, 30 July 2026, still reporting
  NEVER_SUBSCRIBED with the sync on and accepts_marketing true.

Playbook shipped: sort every customer by what you may send them before building anything; route
order news off the marketing path into Shopify's own notifications or a connected email service;
watch the workflow run rather than the inbox and alert on an exception rate; ask for consent at the
moment it is earned, a week after a good delivery rather than mid-checkout; make one deliberate
decision about the pending queue instead of leaving it to grow; reconcile Shopify's consent record
against the email tool's by address; and publish the share of buyers you can reach as a monthly
number. Honest limits stated in the post: it cannot manufacture consent, it cannot confirm a
pending subscriber on their behalf, and it will not make someone who unsubscribed reachable again.

Overlap handled deliberately: Nº 120 (workflows that fail silently) owns Flow's reporting in
general and Nº 113 (order confirmations that never arrive) owns transactional delivery failures.
This post links to both in prose and keeps its weight on the consent gate itself, the exception the
gate produces, and the audience arithmetic behind it.
