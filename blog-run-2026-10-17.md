## New post

**Nº 130 - "How to stop Shopify B2B prices dropping every time you run a sale"**
`how-to-stop-shopify-b2b-prices-dropping-when-you-run-a-sale.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-17 - 2,780 words - 9 min read

Pain point: a retail markdown silently cuts wholesale prices. A B2B catalog percentage is computed
from the product's live store price, so every sale moves the trade floor down with it, and if the
sale is an automatic discount instead, the discount lands on top of the already discounted catalog
price. In a blended store, discounts apply to B2B by default. There is no setting that anchors a
trade price to list price, and no Flow trigger that fires on a price change.

Why this topic: high commercial pain, almost no real content on the open web. Everything written
about Shopify B2B pricing is agency setup guides and app landing pages. Nobody has written about
the pricing-system versus discount-system split as the root cause, nobody has connected the fixed
price escape hatch to the staleness problem it creates, and nobody has written about the catalog
CSV export omitting exactly the prices that move. Audience is B2B and Plus merchants, so the
average order value behind the search is high.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, Creating and managing B2B catalogs: a catalog can apply an overall percentage
  increase or decrease, and the percentage applies to the product's standard store price. Worked
  example: ten dollar candles become eight dollars for companies with catalog access. "Fixed prices
  override any overall adjustments that you set", and a nine dollar fixed price stays nine while
  the rest of the catalog drops to eight. Volume pricing supports up to 10 quantity breaks. A
  company location can have up to 25 catalogs, a store up to 10,000, and Basic/Grow/Advanced are
  capped at 3 active catalogs. Where several catalogs assigned directly to a location include the
  same product at different prices, the lowest price is displayed, ties going to the catalog
  created first. Exported CSV files include only fixed-price products, not percentage-adjusted
  prices. Leaving both Fixed Price and Compare At blank in a CSV import removes that product's
  fixed price.
- Help Center, Discounts for B2B catalogs: "After a fixed price is assigned, any overall adjustments
  on the catalog no longer apply." Automatic discounts and discount codes "apply as additional
  discounts on top of any price adjustments" made through catalogs. Discounts are on by default for
  B2B. In a blended store, existing discounts "apply to both B2B and D2C customers by default", and
  limiting one to B2B means assigning a B2B market in the discount's eligibility settings.
- Admin GraphQL PriceList: `parent` is documented as "Relative adjustments to other prices";
  `priceListCreate` takes "parent adjustment settings that determine how the system calculates
  prices relative to base prices"; `fixedPricesCount` is "The number of fixed prices on the price
  list"; `prices` can be filtered by origin type, fixed or relative; removing a product's fixed
  prices means "all variant prices revert to the price list's adjustment rules".
- `PriceListCompareAtMode` has exactly two values: ADJUSTED, "The compare at price is adjusted based
  on percentage specified in price list", and NULLIFY, "The compare at prices are set to null unless
  explicitly defined by a fixed price value". Neither changes what the buyer is charged.
- `priceListFixedPricesAdd` creates or updates fixed prices and "replaces any existing fixed prices
  for those variants on the price list", requires `write_products`. Shopify Developer Community,
  25 Aug 2025 (Luke) asking whether there is a per-request limit; 26 Aug 2025, Liam-Shopify: "The
  limit for both should be 250", covering `priceListFixedPricesAdd` and
  `priceListFixedPricesUpdate`.
- Shopify Flow native product triggers: product created, product deleted, product status updated,
  product variant added, product variant deleted, product variant inventory quantity changed,
  product variant out of stock, product variant back in stock. No price changed trigger and no
  general product updated trigger. The only B2B pieces are the company location created trigger and
  the add catalog to company location action, which maps to `catalogContextUpdate`.
- Shopify Community, "Price Catalog b2b", 15 Aug 2024 (IoanaHotaran): asks how to base a B2B catalog
  discount on compare at price rather than price, because price is dynamic in B2C and compare at is
  the fixed list price. 11 Dec 2025 (Everlights1): a 20 percent B2B catalog discount stacking on
  B2C promotions across over 300K SKUs, with export and reimport of fixed prices ruled out at that
  scale. 12 Jan 2026 (AccountingHints): workaround is a Sale Items collection updated weekly by
  hand with an automatic discount scoped to it. 28 Aug 2026 (ChrisChu5): set fixed B2B overrides at
  compare-at times 0.8. No Shopify staff replies; several replies promote the replier's own app.
- Shopify Community, "B2B Trade catalog discount stacking from B2C site", 11 Dec 2025 (Everlights1):
  "the discount gets stacked on top of the 20% trade discount on our B2B site". 12 Dec 2025
  (TheUntechnickle): no native fix. 12 Dec 2025 (Everlights1): no dev team, asks for setup
  instructions, and does not post again. 16 Jan 2026 (tobebuilds, Tobe Osakwe): catalogs are part
  of the pricing system, not the discount system. 30 Jun 2026 (sophia24): a Shopify Function
  approach that skips products already on sale. 3 Aug 2026 (souffhy95): asks whether the original
  poster solved it. 4 Aug 2026 (Gaspar_Medina_Burgar): catalog percentage adjustments use the
  current price and `PriceListCompareAtMode` does not change this, so automate fixed catalog prices
  via the Admin GraphQL API or build a custom Function. No staff replies, nothing marked as the
  solution.
- Shopify Community, 11 Jun 2025 (ChloeFrankc), "Fixed Market Price Columns in CSV Not Working After
  Summer '25 Update (Markets to Catalogs)": market price columns behaving inconsistently after the
  change, some products pricing correctly per market and others applying the base price everywhere.
  Same day (halte): the same problem, "had been selling at wrong prices", fixed only by overwriting
  the catalog with an extra import. 16 Aug 2025 (TFF001): across 23 markets and over 1.5M lines, a
  download took more than two hours and produced 23 files totalling 223MB, and they planned to build
  their own export tool. Support said they had not seen the issue before. No staff reply, unresolved.

Playbook shipped: resolve what every buyer actually pays per variant per catalog with cost beside
it, across percentage and fixed prices together; set the trade floor from list price and cost rather
than the retail selling price; write a fixed price to hold that floor when a scheduled markdown or a
price edit would break it; release the fixed price when the markdown ends, 250 variants per call;
refuse any promotion still eligible for a B2B market; alarm on fixed prices older than the last list
price change, on any catalog price under landed cost, and on quantity breaks that stop descending;
and report realized trade margin by company location and by promotion.
