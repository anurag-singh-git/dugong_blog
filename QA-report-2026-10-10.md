# QA report - 2026-10-10

Scheduled autonomous run. Full-tree QA of blog.dugong.live. The daily blog run was still shipping
when this QA started, so the first 25 minutes were spent waiting it out; everything below was
measured against the settled tree (154 files: 79 rendered, 75 stubs) after Dispatch Nº 122 and
Nº 123 landed. Eight defects found and fixed. No facts, FAQ answers or headlines changed.

## Method

- Structural audit of all 154 HTML files: tag balance, one h1 per rendered page, JSON-LD parses,
  internal links and in-page anchors, image sources, canonical, html lang, viewport, meta
  description length and uniqueness, title uniqueness, duplicate ids, replacement characters,
  malformed entities, doubled words, stub noindex/canonical.
- Headless Chromium render of all 79 rendered pages at 320, 360, 375, 414, 768, 1024, 1280 and
  1920 px: horizontal overflow, elements off-screen, text under 13 px, broken images, tap-target
  size at phone widths, console and page errors.
- WCAG AA contrast check on every text node of all 79 pages against its resolved background.
- Word count of every article body measured against the figure the page states, in the ticker,
  the BlogPosting JSON-LD and the homepage graph.
- Coverage cross-check: sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt against the file tree.
- Link graph: inbound and outbound internal links per page, stub redirect targets.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **Homepage newsletter block was clipped on narrow phones.** At 320 px the signup card's
   contents measured 274 px against a 200 px content box: the title, lede, email field and
   subscribe button all ran 35 px past the right edge of the screen, hidden by the card's
   `overflow: hidden` rather than scrolling. Cause was a `1fr` grid column whose automatic
   minimum is the e-mail input's intrinsic width. Both the two-column rule and the ≤760 px
   single-column rule now use `minmax(0, 1fr)`. Verified: the whole card fits at 320 px.

2. **The homepage essay overstated its own length by 4x.** Dispatch Nº 47 ("The death of the
   drag-and-drop builder") claimed "14 MIN READ · 3,420 WORDS" in the ticker and "14 MIN READ"
   in the article meta and the hero card, with `wordCount` 893 in JSON-LD. The body is 819 words.
   Corrected to 4 min / 820 words in all five places.

3. **Dispatch Nº 122 linked twice to an abandoned draft.** The new wholesale tax-exemption post
   sent both a body link and a "read next" card for "The quote that goes quiet" to
   `how-to-send-payment-reminders-for-unpaid-draft-orders-on-shopify.html`, which is one of the
   two noindex, unwired drafts, instead of the published Nº 101 at
   `how-to-automatically-follow-up-on-unpaid-draft-orders-on-shopify.html`. The card also carried
   the draft's date and dek. Both links repointed and the card replaced with the one the other 77
   posts use. No page now links to either orphan.

4. **Stated word counts were wrong on 29 posts.** Thirteen were off by more than 10 percent
   (worst: 1,880 claimed against 2,254 actual) and the rest either passed 5 percent or disagreed
   between the visible ticker figure and the JSON-LD `wordCount`. Each page's two ticker figures,
   its `wordCount` and the matching entry in the homepage graph now carry the measured body count
   rounded to the nearest ten. Read times were left alone: the site has never used a fixed words
   per minute (the spread across 79 posts is 189 to 287), and every corrected page stays inside
   that range.

5. **Tap targets below the comfortable minimum on phones.** Footer column links rendered 24 px
   tall, the "back to field notes" link 27 px and the homepage filter pills 31 px. A mobile-only
   block (marked "QA 2026-10-10") raises footer links to about 36 px, the back link to 40 px and
   the pills to about 38 px, with the footer column gap reduced so the visual rhythm is unchanged.
   Applied to all 79 rendered pages.

6. **Ticker text was 11 px on phones.** Below the 12 px floor Lighthouse treats as legible. The
   ticker is now 12 px at 560 px and under, with letter-spacing trimmed from 0.16em to 0.13em so
   the marquee loop keeps its length. Desktop is untouched.

7. **UK spellings and a missing apostrophe in a US-English tree.** behaviour (8), licence (4),
   summarised (2), organised, optimisation and favour, across seven posts, the homepage, feed.xml,
   llms.txt and llms-full.txt. Also "as it s the default" in the shipping-restrictions post, three
   places plus llms-full. All normalised. "analyses" was left: it is correct US English.

8. **Three defects of record on the two newest posts.** Nº 123's meta description ran 176
   characters against a site median of 156 and a 165 ceiling; trimmed to 158 and mirrored into
   og:description, twitter:description and the homepage graph entry. Nº 122 and Nº 123 titled
   themselves "&middot; Dugong Field Notes" where the other 76 pages use "| Dugong Field Notes";
   so did the personalization post. All three now match.

Also fixed on the homepage: `role="main"` appeared on both the home view and the post view, which
is invalid (one main landmark per document); the post view is now `role="article"`. And the Nº 47
BlogPosting pointed at `https://blog.dugong.live/#post`, an anchor that does not exist; it now
points at `#view-post`, the id the featured card already links to.
