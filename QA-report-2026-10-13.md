# QA report - 2026-10-13

Scheduled autonomous run. Full-tree QA of blog.dugong.live.

**The tree moved under this run again.** The opening snapshot at 16:29 UTC had 168 HTML files
(88 rendered, 80 stubs). Partway through, the blog-generation session published dispatch Nº 126,
`how-to-catch-shopify-shipping-label-adjustments-before-they-eat-your-margin.html`, plus its stub,
and wired it into sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt and 81 inbound links. The audit was
re-run against the settled 170-file tree (89 rendered, 81 stubs) and all figures below are that
second pass. Nothing was clobbered in either direction: every edit here was a read-modify-write
against the live file. Six defects found and fixed. No facts, FAQ answers or headlines were changed.

## Method

- Structural audit of all 170 HTML files: tag and CSS brace balance, one h1 per rendered page,
  JSON-LD parses and field completeness, internal link targets (root-absolute paths resolved against
  the site root, as they are on the live domain), in-page anchors, asset references, canonical vs
  og:url, og:title vs h1, og vs twitter card parity, html lang, viewport, charset, meta description
  length and uniqueness, title length and uniqueness, the `| Dugong` title-suffix rule, duplicate
  ids, replacement characters and mojibake, unterminated entities, heading-level skips, img alt,
  stub noindex and redirect resolution, visible date vs JSON-LD datePublished, visible word figure
  vs JSON-LD wordCount, JSON-LD headline vs h1, dispatch-number collisions, visible em and en
  dashes, UK spellings.
- Headless Chromium render of all 89 rendered pages at 320, 360, 375, 414, 768, 1024, 1280 and
  1920 px (712 renders per pass, four passes): horizontal page overflow, elements escaping the
  viewport, computed font size of every text-bearing element, broken images, tap-target height at
  phone widths, console and page errors.
- WCAG AA contrast on every text node of all 89 pages at all eight widths, each measured against its
  resolved composited background with alpha blending through ancestors.
- New this run: a wrapped-flex-row audit. Every wrapping flex row on every page at five widths was
  split into its rendered visual lines and checked for a decorative separator glyph stranded at the
  start or end of a line. This is what turned up defect 3.
- Doubled-word check run per text node rather than across the flattened document, which removes the
  46 cross-tag false positives (`...on Shopify</a> <a>Shopify Flow...`) that a naive pass reports.
- Spelling pass over every article body, both spellings of every word the tree uses inconsistently,
  with quoted Shopify UI and documentation labels excluded by hand.
- Coverage cross-check: sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt against the file tree;
  sitemap lastmod and feed pubDate sanity, feed ordering, guid uniqueness, XML well-formedness
  before and after every edit.
- Link graph: inbound and outbound internal links per page, stub redirect resolution, orphan check.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **27 pages shipped without the webfonts they are designed in.** Every page declares
   `--display: "Hanken Grotesk"`, `--body: "Inter"` and `--mono: "JetBrains Mono"`, but 26 article
   pages plus the new dispatch never loaded the Google Fonts stylesheet, so they fell straight
   through to `system-ui` while the other 62 rendered in the real typefaces. Two pages of the same
   blog in two different typefaces is the most visible defect the tree has had. All 27 now carry the
   same preconnect pair and the same `css2?family=Hanken+Grotesk...` stylesheet link as the rest,
   inserted immediately before their `<style>` block, byte-identical to the 62 working pages and
   verified inside `<head>` on every one. 404.html, which carries its own narrower weight subset
   (correct: it only uses the weights it requests), was missing just the two preconnect hints; those
   were added for consistency. Coverage is now 89 / 89.

2. **"cancelled" and "canceled" were mixed inside single pages.** 337 uses of `cancelled` against 57
   of `canceled`, and 19 of `cancelling` against 73 of `canceling`, with 14 pages carrying both
   spellings of the same word in the same body copy, including index.html. Normalized to
   `cancelled` / `cancelling`: it is the dominant form, it is what Shopify's own admin, help centre
   and help URLs use (`.../managing-orders/cancelling-orders`), and it is what the tree's own live
   slug `how-to-stop-cancelled-orders-showing-as-unfulfilled-on-shopify.html` already uses, so the
   alternative would have put the prose at odds with the URL. 186 occurrences changed across HTML,
   feed.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt, body copy and JSON-LD twins together so the pairs still
   match. Verified first that no URL, slug, id or class contains either form.
   **The five capitalized `Canceled` on the Flow page are deliberately untouched**: they are a
   verbatim enumeration of Shopify's documented Flow run statuses (In progress, Waiting, Rate
   limited, Canceled, Completed), and a quoted label is reproduced as the vendor spells it.

3. **A separator dot stranded at the end of a wrapped line on 87 of 89 pages, at every phone
   width.** `.article-meta` is a wrapping flex row and each `·` is its own flex item, so when the
   row wrapped the dot was left hanging at the end of the line: "DISPATCH Nº 117 ·" then a line
   break, which reads as though something failed to load. 150 stranded glyphs at 320 and 360 px,
   133 at 375, 87 at 414, none at 768 and above. The dots are decorative and already
   `aria-hidden="true"`, so at ≤560 px they are now hidden and the column gap widens to 1rem, which
   keeps the fields distinct without them. Desktop is untouched and keeps the dots exactly as the
   10-12 run restored them. Re-measured: 0 stranded separators at all five phone widths, and the
   meta row reads "SEP 17, 2026   10 MIN READ" cleanly.

4. **The new dispatch's og:title and twitter:title were the short SEO title, not the h1.** Every
   other article sets og:title and twitter:title to the full h1 and keeps the shortened form for
   `<title>`. Nº 126 had "How to catch Shopify shipping label adjustments early" in all three. The
   two social titles now carry the h1, "How to catch Shopify shipping label adjustments before they
   eat your margin", matching the JSON-LD headline that was already correct.

5. **The new dispatch's meta description was 167 characters, over the 165 cap** the other 88 pages
   hold to. Changed "every" to "each" and dropped one Oxford comma, which brings it to 165 with
   every clause intact. All three copies (description, og:description, twitter:description) were
   changed together.

6. **UK spellings reintroduced since the last run.** `fulfil` x27 as a bare verb, against 247 uses
   of `fulfill` and 737 of `fulfillment` (the 10-12 run fixed `fulfilment` but not the verb);
   `catalogue` x2 against the tree's `catalog`; and `maths` x3 in the new dispatch, against 100+
   uses of `math`, which the 10-12 run had cleared. All now US English. Tree-wide UK-spelling count
   back to zero.

## Standing items (not changed)

1. index.html and 404.html keep an og:title and og:description that differ from their h1 and meta
   description. Both are hand-written site-level blurbs well inside the length caps.
2. Every headless render logs exactly three console errors, and all three are artifacts of rendering
   from `file://`: two root-absolute favicon paths (`/favicon.svg`, `/favicon.png`) that resolve
   correctly on the live domain, and the Google Fonts stylesheet, which this sandbox's proxy blocks.
   The count rising from two to three per render is itself the confirmation that defect 1 is fixed:
   the font request is now attempted on all 89 pages. Because the proxy blocks fonts.gstatic.com,
   the screenshots in this run show fallback type; the fix was verified by markup identity against
   the 62 pages that were already correct, not by eye.
3. "deciding what a customer is is not a slot to fill" in the repeat-customers post is grammatical
   (`what a customer is` + `is not`) but makes a reader stumble. Left as written; rewriting body
   copy is not something an unattended run should do on style grounds alone.
4. Micro-labels (tag pills at 10.24 px, card dates and byline avatars at ~11 px, footer headings and
   the ticker at 11.52 px) still render under 12 px at 768 px and wider. Deliberate editorial
   typography; the 12 px floor is a mobile rule and desktop was left alone.
5. The homepage ticker is an infinite marquee with no pause control. It is `aria-hidden="true"` and
   the tree honors `prefers-reduced-motion`, so it was left as is. Its spans overflow their
   `overflow:hidden` container by design; this run's escape detector is now ancestor-aware and no
   longer reports them, which is what the 3,538 "escapes" in the first pass turned out to be.
6. Inline links inside body copy render 21 px tall at phone widths. WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 exempts
   targets constrained by the line-height of surrounding text.
7. **The 37 distinct external links could not be verified.** Both this sandbox and the desktop shell
   are behind proxies that refuse help.shopify.com, shopify.dev and wikipedia.org. Two were
   spot-checked through a different path: the email-rewrites link resolves to exactly the right
   article, but `help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/cancelling-orders` appears
   to redirect up to its parent "Managing orders" rather than landing on the cancelling section. It
   is not a 404 and guessing a replacement URL unattended is worse than leaving it, so it is flagged
   here rather than changed. A supervised run with open egress should sweep all 37.
8. **Nothing is committed.** The working tree is now well over a hundred files ahead of HEAD: the
   10-12 run's hub pages and feed rewiring, the 10-12 QA fixes, the new dispatch Nº 126, and this
   run's six fixes. None of it is live. The push is still left for a supervised run, for the same
   reason as last time: this session cannot delete files in the folder, which makes git lock-file
   handling unsafe unattended.
   `git add -A && git commit -m "QA 10-13: webfonts on 27 pages, spelling, mobile meta row" && git push`
