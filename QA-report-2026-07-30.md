In `how-to-generate-shopify-product-descriptions-with-ai.html` ("checking each new description
against its neighbours"). The site is American English throughout and uses `neighbor` in seven
other places across five files and llms.txt, so this was unambiguous drift. The sentence does not
appear in that post's JSON-LD, llms.txt dek, or feed description, so one edit was sufficient; all
surfaces stay in sync. Post-fix count of `neighbour*` across all HTML, llms.txt and feed.xml: 0.

`cancellation` ×30+ (correct American English; only `cancelled`/`cancelling` are British — the
site correctly uses `canceled` ×21), `analyses` (plural of analysis, in the order-editing post),
`fulfill`/`fulfillment` (American double-L), and `&ordm;` glyphs the entity scan surfaced.
