## New post

**Nº 134 - "How to find Shopify products missing images or weights"**
`how-to-find-shopify-products-missing-images-or-weights.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-24 -
3,258 words - 10 min read

Pain point: a product goes live without a photo, without a weight, or without a barcode, and nothing
in the Shopify admin will tell you. The products list has sixteen filters and every one of them
selects on a value a product has rather than one it lacks. The CSV export answers the image and
barcode questions and loses the weight question, because a blank weight imports and exports as 0, so
"nobody weighed this" and "this weighs nothing" are the same row. Flow can guard the front door and
has no Product updated trigger, so deleting the last image on a live product fires nothing. And the
condition most merchants write first reports clean on exactly the products it was built to catch.

Why this topic: high demand, almost nothing written, and three live threads across five years with no
Shopify answer on any of them. The open web has app marketing, a few vendor checklists, and one
Shopify help page that says the filters cannot do this and then changes the subject. Nobody has
written down that the empty-list condition silently inverts the result of the standard Flow check,
that the community's accepted field for that check is now deprecated on the Product object, that
MediaStatus makes "has an image" and "has an image that displays" two different questions, or that
the CSV cannot express an unset weight while the Admin API can.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, Searching for products and filtering your product list: sixteen filters, Vendors, Tag,
  Statuses, Categories, Sales channel, Region catalog, B2B catalog, Company location catalog, Retail
  catalog, Unassigned catalog, Types, Collection, Publishing error, Gift card, Combined listings and
  Metafields. The page states that the search bar and the filters do not support searching for
  products that are missing a specific attribute, and for the one case it addresses, products with no
  tags, it recommends exporting the product list and reading the column.
- Help Center, Using CSV files to import and export products: the product image URL column is "the
  URL for the product image", which "Shopify downloads during the import and re-uploads"; the barcode
  column is "all of the barcodes, up to a maximum of 20, for the product or variant", with an untyped
  barcode "imported as a `Custom` barcode"; the weight column is "the weight of the product or
  variant, expressed in grams" and "the default value is `0` if no value is entered". That last line
  is the article's central fact.
- Help Center, Shopify Flow triggers: the product triggers are Product created, Product deleted,
  Product status updated, Product variant added, Product variant deleted, Product variant back in
  stock, Product variant out of stock and Product variant inventory quantity changed. There is no
  Product updated trigger on the page.
- Help Center, Product created: starts "when a user in your organization or a third-party app adds a
  new product" in the admin or via API, "whether or not it is added to the Online Store sales
  channel"; "This trigger only applies to new products" and "It does not start when a new variant is
  added to an existing product"; it "allows actions to be taken with the Product Graphql API object".
- Shopify.dev, Product object: `images` and `featuredImage` are both marked deprecated; `media` is
  the live field, "The media associated with the product. Valid media are images, 3D models, videos."
- Shopify.dev, MediaStatus: four values, UPLOADED "Media has been uploaded but not yet processed",
  PROCESSING "Media is being processed", READY "Media is ready to be displayed", FAILED "Media
  processing has failed". The Media interface carries `status`, `mediaErrors` and `mediaWarnings`.
- Shopify.dev, InventoryItem and InventoryItemMeasurement: `measurement` is `InventoryItemMeasurement!`,
  non-null, "The packaging dimensions of the inventory item"; the `weight` inside it is typed `Weight`
  with no non-null marker, so null and 0 are distinguishable through the API and not through the CSV.
- Shopify Community, 26 April 2023 (JoeWardMenswear), "How can I add a no-image filter to my
  products?": asks for a filter for products with no image and lists the filters he does have. No
  Shopify staff reply, never marked resolved, and the original poster never posted again. On 15
  January 2026, two years and nine months later, marketingnep added that he runs a Flow tagging new
  products `missing_image` and still cannot find the ones created before that flow existed, and had
  resorted to a Python exporter from GitHub. PaulNewton suggested looping the existing products
  through the flow or using Mechanic. Still unresolved.
- Shopify Community, 16 January 2024 (BigLaCroix), "I want to write a flow that checks to see if a
  product has an image": over 2,000 items, many unphotographed, staff creating duplicate listings
  without images; unsure between featuredImage, featuredMedia, images and media. paul_n advised
  Product > Images > URL and to leave Media alone; confirmed working the same evening. On 16 April
  2024 SGMuseum built the same flow and it never fired on a test product with no image; paul_n
  explained that with no images only the Any/All/None operator runs, recommended None with "None of
  product / images is not empty or exists" and switching the action paths; confirmed working. No
  Shopify staff reply. This thread is the source of the article's central mechanism.
- Shopify Community, 29 September 2021 (ReneK), "Admin API (REST or GraphQL): List Products/Variants
  with no Images": tens of thousands of products uploaded nightly, wanted to list the ones with no
  images before customers noticed. Answered himself the same afternoon: check the response after
  creating a product and, if an expected image is missing, set the product to draft and send an
  email. VivekH on 5 October suggested checking whether the uploaded media had been processed and
  using the status to flag; marked as the accepted solution on 6 October. No Shopify staff reply.
- Shopify Community, 10 August 2026 (CommerceGov), "How do Shopify agencies verify product images
  after bulk catalog changes?": replies converge on comparing intended state against live state
  rather than before against after, recording ids, image references, positions, alt text and variant
  assignments; treating a successful write as evidence only that the operation finished; and, from
  Ian_Chechin on 30 August, waiting before verifying because image processing is asynchronous, with
  only the delayed result treated as valid. Two replies carry vendor disclosures and are used for the
  shape of the advice rather than for any product claim. No accepted answer.

Playbook shipped: write down what finished means in your store; check media status, not whether media
exists; treat a zero weight as unknown, not as zero; hold the publish instead of reporting the
damage; sweep the back catalog once, because no trigger covers it; re-check after the delay, because
processing is asynchronous; send one list every morning, worst first. Honest limits stated in the
post: the automation cannot weigh the boots or photograph the sofa, only refuse to let an unweighed
and unphotographed product sell; a forty-product catalog is faster to fix in a spreadsheet; and a
gate needs somebody nominated in advance who is allowed to override it.
