# QA report - 2026-10-15

Scheduled autonomous run. Full-tree QA of blog.dugong.live.

**The tree moved under this run again.** The opening snapshot at 16:14 UTC had 175 HTML files
(93 rendered, 82 stubs). At 16:30 the blog-generation session published dispatch Nº 128,
`how-to-stop-reordering-shopify-stock-you-already-have-on-order.html`, and rewired sitemap.xml,
feed.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, index.html, two hub pages and the author page. The audit was
re-run from scratch against the settled 176-file tree (94 rendered, 82 stubs) once the run finished
at 16:31, and every figure below is that second pass. Five defects found and fixed, three of them on
the dispatch that landed mid-run. Nothing was clobbered in either direction: every edit was a
read-modify-write against the live file, each one guarded by an exact-match assertion that aborts
the whole patch rather than writing a near-miss. No facts, FAQ answers, headlines or body copy were
changed.

**New this run: the pages were rendered in their real typefaces for the first time.** Every previous
run measured layout against `system-ui`, because both sandboxes block fonts.googleapis.com, and said
so as a standing caveat. This run installed Inter, Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono from the npm
registry (which is reachable), served them from a local origin, and intercepted the Google Fonts
request so the real faces load. Chromium confirms the faces resolve
(`document.fonts.check('700 2rem "Hanken Grotesk"')` is true). Every layout, overflow, font-size and
tap-target figure below is therefore measured on the typography the site actually ships, not on a
fallback with different metrics.

## Method

- Structural audit of all 176 HTML files: tag and CSS brace balance, one h1 per rendered page, JSON-LD
  parses and field completeness, internal link targets, in-page anchors, asset references, canonical
  vs og:url, og:title vs h1, og vs twitter parity, html lang, viewport, charset, meta description
  length and uniqueness, title length and uniqueness, the `| Dugong` title-suffix rule, duplicate ids,
  replacement characters and mojibake, unterminated entities, heading-level skips, img alt, stub
  noindex and redirect resolution, visible date vs JSON-LD datePublished, visible word figure vs
  JSON-LD wordCount, JSON-LD headline vs h1, dispatch-number collisions and gaps, visible em and en
  dashes, UK spellings.
- Headless Chromium render of all 94 rendered pages at 320, 360, 375, 414, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1920 px
  with the real webfonts loaded (752 renders per pass, three passes): horizontal page overflow,
  elements escaping the viewport, computed font size of every text-bearing element, broken images,
  tap-target height at phone widths, stranded separator glyphs in wrapped flex rows, console and page
  errors.
- WCAG AA contrast on every text node of all 94 pages at all eight widths, each measured against its
  resolved composited background with alpha blending through ancestors.
- New this run: a **scroll-reveal completeness** pass. The tree hides `.card`, `.section-head` and
  every `.article-body` paragraph, heading, `pre`, callout and blockquote behind `opacity: 0` and
  reveals them with an IntersectionObserver, so a reveal that never fires is invisible body copy. All
  94 pages were scrolled end to end at reader pace and every `[class*=reveal]` element re-measured.
  Also checked with JavaScript disabled and under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- New this run: a **cross-page card-metadata** pass. Every card anywhere in the tree was compared
  against the page it links to, on reading time, tag, date label and `datetime` attribute. This is
  what turned up defects 4 and 5.
- Content pass: dispatch numbering sequence and gaps, declared `wordCount` against the real body text
  of every post, reading time against word count, every FAQ question in the JSON-LD checked for a
  matching visible question, BreadcrumbList presence, index hero and stats against the newest post.
- Spelling pass over 22 US/UK pairs, per page and tree-wide, with quoted Shopify UI labels excluded by
  hand. Doubled-word check run per text node rather than across the flattened document.
- Coverage cross-check: sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt against the file tree;
  sitemap lastmod and feed pubDate sanity, feed ordering, guid uniqueness, XML well-formedness before
  and after every edit.
- Link graph: inbound and outbound internal links per page, stub redirect resolution, orphan check.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **The homepage masthead was a day and a dispatch stale.** The blog run wrote index.html at 16:29
   and published Nº 128 at 16:30, so the recent-dispatches grid, the topic cloud and the Blog JSON-LD
   all carried the new post correctly, but the four hand-maintained numbers above them did not. The
   hero read `ISSUE Nº 127` and `OCT 14 · 2026` directly beside a pulsing "LATEST DISPATCH" label that
   was pointing at yesterday; the published-dispatches stat read 127; and the ticker, which appears
   twice, read "6 NEW DISPATCHES THIS MONTH" against seven October posts. Checked against eight
   commits of history first to confirm the invariant the page holds to (hero issue = latest dispatch
   number, hero date = that dispatch's date, stat = same number, ticker = that month's post count);
   all four now read 128, OCT 15 · 2026, 128 and 7.

2. **Nº 128's meta description was 201 characters, far over the 165 cap** the other 93 pages hold to,
   and long enough for Google to truncate it mid-clause. Rewritten to 162 characters with the
   pain-point sentence intact: the trailing "and chases the late ones" is the only thing dropped, and
   nothing else in the clause moved. All three copies (description, og:description,
   twitter:description) were changed together so the triple still matches.

3. **Nº 128 shipped without its BreadcrumbList.** Its JSON-LD `@graph` was BlogPosting, FAQPage, HowTo
   where all 91 other article pages carry BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, HowTo. Added in the
   same position as its siblings, with Field Notes → Shopify inventory automation → the post, the hub
   taken from the two hub pages that actually list it. Position 3 uses the h1, because this post has
   no short editorial title anywhere in the tree (all three cards that link to it use the h1) and
   inventing one is an editorial decision, not a QA fix. JSON-LD re-parsed clean afterwards.

4. **Nine related-article cards advertised the wrong reading time.** Three pages'
   "READ NEXT" blocks disagreed with the pages they link to: the restrict-shipping post promised 10
   min for a 7 min article, 10 for a 9 and 12 for an 8; the returns-never-sent-back post promised 6
   for a 10, 7 for a 9 and 7 for a 10; the price-adjustment post promised 6 for a 10, 6 for a 10 and
   6 for a 9. In every one of the nine cases the rest of the tree was right and the single card was
   wrong, by 40 to 67 agreeing references each, so the targets' own figures were taken as truth. Card
   dates and `datetime` attributes were checked the same way across the whole tree and were already
   correct everywhere.

5. **Three cards filed the automations guide under the wrong tag.** The pickup-orders, vacation-mode
   and expiry-dates posts each labelled their card for `best-shopify-automations-to-set-up.html` as
   `tactics`, against 60 cards elsewhere and the page's own `article:section` and byline, all of which
   say `research`. The wrong class also gave those three pills the wrong colour. Changed to
   `tag tag-research` / `research`.

## Standing items (not changed)

1. index.html, 404.html and priya-singh.html keep an og:title or og:description that differs from
   their h1 and meta description. All are hand-written page-level blurbs well inside the length caps.
   priya-singh.html is new to this list only because the author page is new to the tree; its
   og:title "Priya Singh, Shopify operations research" against an h1 of "Priya Singh" is the same
   deliberate pattern as the other two.
2. Every headless render logs three console errors, all artifacts of the sandbox: two root-absolute
   favicon paths that resolve on the live domain, and the Google Fonts stylesheet, which the proxy
   blocks. The font request is now answered locally, so the typography is verified; the logged error
   is the blocked upstream request, not a site fault.
3. The three capitalized `Canceled` on the Flow page are still deliberate and still untouched: they
   are a verbatim enumeration of Shopify's documented Flow run statuses, and a quoted label is
   reproduced as the vendor spells it. Tree-wide the prose is `cancelled` / `cancelling`, 247 to 0.
4. Two doubled words survive, both grammatical: "deciding what a customer is is not a slot to fill"
   in the repeat-customers post, and "double opt-in in particular" in the fake-accounts post. Neither
   is an error. Rewriting body copy on style grounds is not something an unattended run should do.
5. Micro-labels (tag pills at 10.24 px, card dates and bylines at ~11 px, footer headings and the
   ticker at 11.52 px) still render under 12 px at 768 px and wider. Deliberate editorial typography;
   the 12 px floor is a mobile rule and desktop was left alone. All of them clear AA contrast.
6. The homepage ticker is an infinite marquee with no pause control. It is `aria-hidden="true"` and
   the tree honors `prefers-reduced-motion`, so it was left as is.
7. Reading times are editorial, not computed: across 82 posts they run 172 to 300 words per minute
   around a median of 250, and no single formula fits more than a third of them. The slowest,
   `how-to-handle-high-risk-orders-on-shopify.html` at 1,550 words over 9 minutes, is the far end of
   that spread rather than a separate kind of thing, and its declared wordCount matches its real body
   text. Left as written. If a consistent figure is wanted, that is a decision for a supervised run.
8. **The 37 distinct external links still could not be verified.** Both the cloud sandbox and the
   desktop shell are behind proxies that refuse help.shopify.com, shopify.dev, wikipedia.org and
   dugong.live; every one returns a connection refusal, not a 404. The
   `help.shopify.com/.../cancelling-orders` redirect flagged on 10-13 is still unconfirmed for the
   same reason. A supervised run with open egress should sweep all 37.
9. **Nothing is committed, and two dispatches are not live.** HEAD is `8e5c9e3` from 02:19 on 10-15,
   which predates both the 10-14 and 10-15 blog runs. The working tree is 106 entries ahead: 93
   modified files and 13 untracked, among them `how-to-catch-shopify-orders-where-half-the-order-never-ships.html`
   (Nº 127), `how-to-stop-reordering-shopify-stock-you-already-have-on-order.html` (Nº 128),
   `priya-singh.html`, `shopify-automation-questions.html`,
   `the-death-of-the-drag-and-drop-builder.html` and one stub. Readers on blog.dugong.live are two
   dispatches behind. The push is still left for a supervised run, for the same reason as before:
