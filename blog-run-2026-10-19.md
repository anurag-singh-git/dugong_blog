## New post

**Nº 132 - "How to stop shipping Shopify orders that say Payment pending"**
`how-to-stop-shipping-shopify-orders-with-payment-pending.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-10-19 - 2,849 words - 9 min read

Pain point: an order whose payment status reads Payment pending looks identical to a paid order in the
unfulfilled queue. It gets picked, packed, labelled and shipped, and about a week later the status turns
to Expired. Shopify never blocks fulfillment on payment, its documented pre-fulfillment gate checks risk
assessment and inventory availability only, the automatic fulfillment setting carries a fraud override and
no payment condition at all, and the only guidance is a sentence asking a human to wait for Paid.

Why this topic: high commercial pain, very little written. Everything on the open web about Shopify
pending payments is either "what does payment pending mean" explainer copy or subscription dunning content.
Nobody has written that Unpaid is a documented grouping of four different risks, so the filter that looks
like the answer puts Authorized (safe to ship) next to Pending (not safe); nobody has pointed out that
Shopify already defines AWAITING_PAYMENT as a fulfillment hold reason and never applies it; nobody has
connected the documented exclusions on the Order paid trigger to the Collect Payment reports, which is the
reason the obvious Flow comes apart on release rather than on hold. Searchers arrive on "shopify payment
pending", "shopify order pending payment fulfill" and "shopify payment status expired" with goods at risk.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, Pending payments: the status applies to payments made by cryptocurrency, wire, barcodes
  "or other alternatives to credit card payments", set when the provider has started processing but needs
  more time, and processing can take "several days" depending on payment type. You are not prevented from
  fulfilling these orders, and the instruction is to "wait until the payment status is Paid before
  fulfilling" to make sure you get paid. Pending payments "usually have an expiry date of about a week",
  with the exact expiry held by the provider. On failure the customer gets an email with a Pay now link to
  retry; on success a confirmation; if not completed by the expiry date the order status becomes Expired.
  While pending you may not be able to edit items, discounts or the shipping address, restock items, cancel
  the order, manually collect payment or mark the order as paid; the restrictions lift when the status
  changes. Manual payment gateways send no email, so the order timeline is the only record. Before
  cancelling an expired order, contact the provider in case the payment can still be captured.
- Help Center, Order status: Unpaid is defined as "a grouping that includes Authorized, Pending, Expired,
  and Partially paid". Authorized means the provider verified the details and funds are held until capture.
  Expired means the capture window passed, and some alternative providers use it when they could not
  process a payment. Due means a payment on a pending order is past its due date. On hold means fulfillment
  is paused by you or an app, inventory can be reserved, and the order cannot be fulfilled until released.
- Help Center, Order processing: the options are "Automatically fulfill the order's line items" and
  "Automatically fulfill all orders, even those with a high risk of fraud". No payment condition exists on
  either. Automatic archiving applies to orders that are paid for and fulfilled.
- Help Center, Deferred payments: orders awaiting payment show Payment pending, and the status becomes
  Overdue if payment is not received by the due date.
- Help Center, Fulfillment order ready to fulfill (Flow): starts a workflow "when an order has completed
  risk assessment and has inventory available for the order", after Shopify releases its initial hold.
  Payment is not a condition.
- Help Center, Hold fulfillment (Flow): changes the order status to On hold, takes a Reason and Reason
  notes, can notify the merchant. Order created is a compatible order level trigger, and order level
  triggers hold every fulfillment order on the order.
- Help Center, Order paid (Flow): starts when payment status changes to paid. Partially paid orders "do not
  start a workflow" with it, and authorized but uncaptured funds do not either.
- Admin GraphQL, FulfillmentHoldReason: eight values, including AWAITING_PAYMENT, "The fulfillment hold is
  applied because payment is pending.", alongside HIGH_RISK_OF_FRAUD, INVENTORY_OUT_OF_STOCK,
  INCORRECT_ADDRESS, AWAITING_RETURN_ITEMS, ONLINE_STORE_POST_PURCHASE_CROSS_SELL, UNKNOWN_DELIVERY_DATE
  and OTHER.
- Admin GraphQL, orderPaymentStatus: "Retrieves the status of a deferred payment by its payment reference
  ID", used to "monitor the processing status of payments that are initiated through payment mutations",
  returning status, paymentReferenceId, errorMessage, translatedErrorMessage and transactions. Deferred
  payments are called payment terms in the API.
- Shopify Changelog, 19 June 2024, "Changes to Shopify Collective supplier order payment status": orders
  received from retailers who do not use automatic payments "will be marked as 'Payment Pending', not
  'Paid'".
- Help Center, Shopify Collective supplier payments: with automatic payments off the status is always
  Payment pending; Payment pending means money has not yet been debited from the retailer's Shopify
  Payments balance; funds do not transfer to the supplier until after fulfillment; ship promptly once an
  order is Paid and do not ship orders in Payment pending status.
- Shopify Community, 9 October 2026 (Omar_Emam), "Shopify Collective suppliers: how are you preventing
  orders from shipping before payment is secured?": an order "can still be marked Payment Pending while
  fulfillment workflows" treat it as ready to process, manual checks do not scale, and a single missed
  status check ships before payment is secured. No replies on the thread.
- Shopify Community, 18 April 2022 (bo2country), "Order Paid trigger not working for payments completed
  after initial order was created": "Only orders manually marked as 'Paid' starts the 'Order Paid' trigger
  automation", payments taken later through Collect Payment did not fire it, and "This did not use to behave
  this way previously". Shopify staff (G-L) replied on 21 April 2022, "Thanks for reporting this, we'll look
  into it and get back." No later staff answer on the thread.
- Shopify Community, 14 November 2023 (HolaJefe), "How to set flow to look for order with pending payment
  and add specific tag?": the only reply suggests checking displayFinancialStatus for PENDING and querying
  with a financial_status:PENDING filter, notes "It's not clear when you want to do that check", and no
  accepted answer or follow up exists.
- Shopify Community, 10 December 2025, "PayPal always pending and never comes through": "Since December
  anyone who has paid via PayPal on my site their payment is pending and never comes through." One
  non-staff reply suggesting provider-side causes.

Playbook shipped: read the payment status rather than the fulfillment status; hold with AWAITING_PAYMENT
the moment a pending order appears; let authorized and paid orders through untouched; poll the money and
release the hold the hour it lands; count the days to expiry and chase while the Pay now link still works;
flag every release done by hand while payment is pending; close out expired and failed payments the same
day. Honest limits stated in the post: a hold stops fulfillment but not a determined human, a pending order
cannot be edited, restocked, cancelled or marked as paid until Shopify lets go of it, and the real expiry
date belongs to the payment provider, so the clock is an estimate from order creation.
