## Issue 1 fixed: 145 stale READ NEXT card chips across 42 files

The mesh passes that build each post's READ NEXT grid copy cards verbatim from sibling pages.
Over time that propagated stale date and read-time chips: cards whose target post had since been
revised (shorter read time) or whose displayed date never matched the target's datePublished.
Cross-checking every card chip against the target post's own JSON-LD (the same source the index
cards use, and the index JSON-LD agrees) found 145 wrong chips, for example:

| Card target | Card said | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| WISMO tickets | 9 min, JUN 6 | 6 min, JUN 4 |
| best-automations hub | 10 or 12 min, JUN 22 | 8 min, MAY 28 |
| tag orders | 8 min, JUL 3 | 9 min, JUN 18 |
| schedule a sale | 8 min, JUN 27 | 9 min, JUN 16 |

Fix: every `post-read` and `card-date` chip inside every card anchor (1,512 cards site-wide,
posts + hub + index) was rewritten from the target post's schema values. 145 chips changed
across 42 post files. Card titles, deks, and link targets were untouched.

Note: the first scripted pass of this fix contained a replacement bug that duplicated card
bodies and dropped closing anchor tags. Post-edit verification caught it immediately (anchor
open/close counts), and a deterministic repair restored all 1,512 anchors. Final state was
re-verified from scratch: tag balance (a, div, span, p, h3, section, li and others) passes on
all 85 files, all 1,512 card chips now match their targets, zero self-links, zero true
duplicate cards.
