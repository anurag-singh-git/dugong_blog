## New post

**Nº 138 - "How to find Shopify products with no cost per item"**
`how-to-find-shopify-products-with-no-cost-per-item.html` - Priya Singh - 2026-11-02 -
2,786 words - 10 min read

Pain point: a variant with no Cost per item does not show up in Shopify's profit reports as a
loss, or as a zero margin, or as a blank row anybody would notice. The order is excluded from the
report entirely. The result is a clean, plausible profit report of a smaller store, and the only
visible symptom is that the sales total in a profit report does not match the sales total in the
sales reports. Nobody reconciles those two numbers, so the gap can sit for a year.

Why this topic: high demand, almost no written mechanism. The open web on this keyword is app
listings for COGS dashboards and agency posts about why Shopify reporting is limited. Nobody has
put together that the exclusion is documented behaviour rather than a bug, that the cost is
captured at the moment of sale so backfilling fixes nothing historical, that the field sits on the
variant and is not editable per variant while a product with options is being created (which is
the actual reason catalogs are full of blanks), that there is still no filter or sort for cost in
the admin four years after the question was first asked, that wrong costs are more common than
missing ones and pass every completeness check, and that Flow's get-data ceiling of 100 items is
the wrong shape for the one job that has to look at every variant.

Facts used, all checked live this run:
- Help Center, Profit reports: "Profit is reported only for products and variants that had a cost
  recorded at the time they were sold"; "The report only takes into consideration the variants that
  have product cost information at the time of sale"; "If orders are missing from this report, then
  it's likely that these variants don't have cost information"; "The Cost per item field contains
  static data". Five reports named: average profit margin by market, gross profit by product, gross
  profit by product variant, gross profit by POS location, profit margin by order. The page also
  notes the resulting difference between net sales in the sales reports and in the profit reports.
- Help Center, Using CSV files to import and export products: the column is "Cost per item",
  described as "How much it costs you to make or acquire the product or variant", monetary value
  without a currency symbol.
- Help Center, Schedules, get data and looping workflows in Shopify Flow: the Scheduled time
  trigger "starts a workflow at a specific time and date that you specify", recurs hourly, daily,
  weekly or monthly, and "can run as frequently as every 10 minutes"; Get product data "retrieve a
  list of products that have no inventory"; and the ceiling, "Get data actions can only run on
  lists up to 100 items long".
- Shopify Community, 31 March 2022 (Shaugn), "How to find products without a cost per item for
  inventory reporting?": "Is there a way to find all products that do not have a cost per item in a
  search?" TeamSpocket: "As far as I know, there is no way to filter or sort products based on
  product cost," with CSV export as the workaround. OP on 8 April: "We used the export solution
  provided." No staff reply, nothing marked as a solution.
- Shopify Community, 3 April 2024 (bloodyrosebtq), "Cost Per Item No Longer Available For Variant
  When Creating Product": "you were able to edit the Cost Per Item for each variant SKU BEFORE
  saving/creating the product"; "Some products have a different cost depending on size or other
  reason like exclusivity." Victor_5, 12 April 2024: "Thank you for reaching out and for letting us
  know that you'd like to see this flow reinstated," passed to the development team. Nothing marked
  as solved, no timeline.
- Shopify Community, 16 October 2026 (justinjung_tb): "16 of our 29 active products had the wrong
  'cost per item'"; "some were set equal to the price, a few were $999 placeholders"; "Shopify's own
  profit report was telling us we lost money on products that actually make money."
- Shopify Community, 12 October 2026 (juhyun_han), "What is your stock worth?": "Shopify holds the
  quantities and it holds a cost per item field, but it never multiplies the two for you"; "A real
  shop has variants with the cost field empty"; "They can be counted as costing nothing"; "The first
  two give you a number that is wrong in a direction you cannot see." One reply (devcoders) on
  reconciling year-end inventory value with accounting records.
- Shopify Community, 7 September 2026 (juhyun_han), "Shopify keeps one cost per variant. Where do
  you keep the rest?": 18 replies. gotinker: "Cost per item is one number per variant. Shopify will
  overwrite it. There is no delivery history behind that field." Kalvin_Mizzi: "Type a new number in
  and every unit you are holding revalues, including the ones you bought at the old price."
  One-Sun1995: "Cost per item is a single value on the variant's inventory item."
- Shopify Developer Community, 11 April 2025, "Asymmetry of cost and unitCost on InventoryItem":
  InventoryItem exposes `unitCost` for reading and has no `cost` field, while the inventory item
  input carries no unitCost, so the value is written through the variant bulk update instead.

Playbook shipped: sweep every variant rather than every product; flag the blanks the day the
product is created; catch the costs that are obviously wrong as well as the missing ones;
keep a dated cost for every delivery; reconcile profit-report sales against total sales; value the
stock with the gaps shown; hold the launch until a cost is on it. Honest limits stated in the post:
it cannot invent a cost nobody recorded, it cannot repair margin on orders that already shipped
without one, and it can flag a cost that looks wrong but not one that is quietly ten percent stale.

Overlap handled deliberately: Nº 135 (bulk price editing) owns the price side of the margin and
Nº 97 (dead stock) owns the aging side of valuation. This post links to both in prose and keeps its
weight on the cost field itself, the report exclusion and the catalog sweep.
